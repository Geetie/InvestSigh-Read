- p.1｜Meta is considering either selling "raw" compute capacity to external companies or access to various AI models that are hosted on Meta’s existing infrastructure, as per Bloomberg news. We flag that CEO Mark Zuckerberg had previously floated both of these options on various public calls and as such this is not new news. As this new line of business was not part of what Mr. Zuckerberg had previously articulated as long term opportunities (advertising, more engaging experiences, business messaging, Meta AI, AI devices), some investors may be wary of a pivot. With META until this article trading at 17x our 2027 GAAP EPS estimate, our outlook is more pragmatic: we think that either of these developments will plug some of the duration problem that has ailed the stock, as selling cloud capacity or model access will theoretically yield higher near-term revenue versus waiting for Meta Business Agents and Meta AI chatbots to scale. The incremental revenue would hence also alleviate concerns that 2027 EPS will be flat or compress vs 2026 (we are modeling $32.67 / $32.98 in 2026 / 2027 EPS).
