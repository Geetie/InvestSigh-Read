Citi’s Inference Ahead - Segmenting the Frontier
CITI'S TAKE 
With AI spend accelerating beyond the growth in inference supply, we 
expect AI Lab models to continue to leverage pricing segmentation (as with 
the release of Anthropic’s Claude Opus 4.8 (5/28)) to drive realized pricing 
higher on a per token basis ( Figure 7). Anthropic's $65B Series H raise 
(Anthropic, 5/28) coinciding with the leaderboard-topping release of 
Claude Opus 4.8 ( Figure 2) underscores how AI capital and capability are 
compounding simultaneously. The inclusion of all three High Bandwidth 
Memory manufacturers as strategic partners in the raise serves as 
confirmation that memory is expected to remain the most significant 
bottleneck in the AI supply chain.
Demand signals. Despite clear acceleration in enterprise demand, Uber’s COO 
noted AI ROI remains unmeasurable at the feature-output level: "That link is not 
there yet...it's very hard to draw a line between one of those stats and 'Okay now 
we're actually producing like 25% more useful consumer features' " (Rapid Response, 
5/22). The SF Fed's Economic Letter 2026-14 (FRBSF, 5/26) provides some 
confirmation at a macro level. Labor productivity is rising (57% high-growth regime 
probability) while T otal Factor Productivity sits at 21% as of Q4 2025. Using GPU 
rental prices as a proxy, we believe that incremental inference supply coming into the 
market is providing some relief from rising inference costs with H100 rental pricing 
down 10% over the last two weeks while still up 27% since the beginning of the year 
(Figure 10). We believe this will continue as the 35-50x more performant Blackwell 
infrastructure comes online as a larger share of new capacity additions.
Supply constraints. We see model efficiency as a critical tool to address the supply 
constraints that exist across the full chain of AI infrastructure, reflected in NVIDIA's 
$150B Taiwan commitment (CNBC, 5/27) as they attempt to break the logjam 
between chip companies’ demand for capacity and the foundries’ willingness to add 
capacity. Huawei's TAU Scaling Law (Huawei, 5/25) at IEEE ISCAS offers the 
geopolitical counternarrative of an efficiency-first framework for frontier-level AI 
performance.
Frontier shifting. Newer models entering the leaderboard over the last six weeks 
tend to be faster than the models they replace, reflecting a broader industry trend 
toward optimizing inference throughput ( Figure 1) while still leapfrogging in 
intelligence. This is particularly notable as early benchmarking scores for Alibaba's 
Qwen3.7-Max rank Qwen among other frontier models (Figure 2).
Agentic build-out. Cognition AI's Series D (Cognition, 5/27) will fuel their 
autonomous agents to deliver development output beyond the coding assistant 
model. DeepSWE (Datacurve, 5/26) and CursorBench 3.1 (Cursor, 3/11) reveal taskspecific benefits and optimizations across the coding agent landscape ( Figure 10, 
Figure 11), underscoring that frontier competition is increasingly fought on vertical 
benchmarks rather than general leaderboards. Meta launched paid subscription 
tiers (TechCrunch, 5/27) across Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp providing 
momentum in consumer AI, while simultaneously standing up a dedicated 
enterprise push (The Information, 5/27).

expectations of expected total return ("ETR") and risk. ETR is the sum of the forecast price appreciation (or depreciation) plus 
the dividend yield for a stock within the next 12 months.  The target price is based on a 12 month time horizon. The Investment 
rating definitions are: Buy (1) ETR of 15% or more or 25% or more for High risk stocks; and Sell (3) for negative ETR. Any covered

valuation drivers and/or investment catalysts to derive a positive or negative investment view, they may elect with the approval 
of Citi Research management not to assign a target price and, thus, not derive an ETR. Citi Research may suspend its rating 
and target price and assign “Rating Suspended” status for regulatory and/or internal policy reasons. Citi Research may also 
suspend its rating and target price and assign "Under Review" status for other exceptional circumstances (e.g. lack of 
information critical to the analyst's thesis, trading suspension) affecting the company and/or trading in the company's 
securities. In both such situations, the rating and target price will show as “—“ and “-“ respectively in the rating history price 
chart. Prior to 11 April 2022 Citi Research assigned “Under Review” status to both situations and prior to 11 Nov 2020 only in 
exceptional circumstances. As soon as practically possible, the analyst will publish a note re-establishing a rating and 
investment thesis. Investment ratings are determined by the ranges described above at the time of initiation of coverage, a 
change in investment and/or risk rating, or a change in target price (subject to limited management discretion). At times, the 
expected total returns may fall outside of these ranges because of market price movements and/or other short-term volatility
