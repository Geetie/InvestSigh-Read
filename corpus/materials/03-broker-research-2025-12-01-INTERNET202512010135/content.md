Are Surging TPU Supply Checks More Evidence of a Budding TPU Sales Strategy?
TPU supply uncertainty may be less of a question as our Asia semiconductor team now expects ~5mn/~7mn TPUs to be produced in '27/'28, up from ~3mn/3.2mn previously. We note every 500k of TPU chip sales could potentially add ~$13bn of revenue and $.40 to GOOGL EPS in '27.
Could Surging TPU Supply Side Checks Be Tea Leaves of 1P Sales Strategy Ahead? In What Could GOOGL Selling TPUs Externally Mean for GOOGL and Semis? we detailed the implications of GOOGL’s potential strategy to sell TPUs into third party data centers… and how every 500k TPU chip sales could potentially add ~$13bn of revenue and ~$.40 of ‘27 EPS. One of the gating factors we flagged was supply uncertainty… but that bottleneck may potentially be less in question. This is because Charlie Chan, our Asia Semiconductor analyst, has picked up supply chain signal on increased TPU purchasing in ‘27/‘28…which led him to upgrade MediaTek to OW. Please see Stronger than Expected AI ASIC Demand; Upgrade to OW for full detail… but in effect, he now expects ~5mn TPUs to be purchased in ‘27 (up ~67% from previous estimates) and 7mn in ‘28 (up ~120% from prior estimates). While most of this is likely due to GOOGL first party use and GCP sales, 12mn TPUs over 2 years (vs 7.9mn over the previous 4 years) speaks to the potential for GOOGL to sell more TPUs through GCP and as a 1P separate offering.
