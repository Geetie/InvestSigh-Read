What are SMBs Saying about GOOGL, META, and ChatGPT Advertising?
Our SMB survey shows strong adoption and forward intent for Google Search and AI Overviews while Meta remains widely used but shows modest Y/Y softness. Interest to experiment on ChatGPT ads is high and SMB generative text and video creation continues to bud.

Accompanying our joint 19th annual local ad survey (here), we detail key takeaways for the leading digital ad platforms
GOOGL: Search and AI Overviews On the Rise: Search and AI Overviews outperformed other platforms on both adoption and ROAS as GOOGL continues to drive meaningful conversion for advertisers. Google Search saw a 4% uplift in usage since last year and a 13% Y/Y increase in advertiser ROI. AI Overviews continue to impress with a 9% uptick in usage Y/Y as SMBs continue to interact with the new AI-enabled search medium. Strength in Search and AI Overviews confirms GOOGL's position as a relative AI-winner and reiterates the company's ability to drive meaningful conversion results across a variety of advertisers. YouTube results are showing mixed messages with usage and intent lower, but ROI slightly higher Y/Y.

META: Core Facebook and Instagram Usage Remains Robust, but Surprisingly Takes a Step Back Y/Y: Core Facebook remains the top platform used by SMB advertisers, but saw some weakening usage (down ~9% Y/Y) as well as lower ROAS (down ~7% Y/Y). Instagram adoption also moderated with only 57% of advertisers using the platform in 2025, down from 64% the year prior; 6% of advertisers said Instagram provided the highest ROI, down from 10% a year ago. These results are admittedly somewhat surprising given other ad conversations we host speak to high and rising ROAS on META (as engagement has accelerated and ad performance is improving). As such, we intend to monitor whether these data are the beginning of a trend or moreso onetime.

Over 80% of SMBs Indicating An Interest to Spend on ChatGPT Ads: Our survey data are incrementally positive for ChatGPT's early ad offering, as 47%/36% of SMB advertisers said they would be very likely/somewhat likely to spend on ChatGPT ads if the offering were made available. Further, 53% of respondents said spend allocated towards ChatGPT would cannibalize spend currently going towards other ad platforms; in our view, early ad dollars going towards this platform are likely to be experimental in nature, thus we would expect this to be less of a headwind to META/GOOGL, and moreso to the sub-scale ad players including SNAP/PINS/RDDT. The scalability of ChatGPT's ad offering will come down to scaling performancedriven return, but these data are constructive about the intent to experiment.
