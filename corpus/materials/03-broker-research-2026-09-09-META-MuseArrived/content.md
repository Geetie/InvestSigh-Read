Muse Has Arrived; What Matters Next?
META unveiled Muse, its standalone consumer AI agent. The battle to address the $30tn consumer agentic TAM is approaching. Why we think META could win, what we need to see, and what this means for GOOGL and other players downstream. OW; META remains Top Pick.
What Happened? META unveiled its new consumer AI agent called Muse (formerly codename "Hatch") as an agentic offering that helps users automate a variety of tasks including online shopping, travel, buying tickets, scheduling appointments/ managing calendar, sending emails, among other use cases. While the agent is able to perform a variety of tasks autonomously, sensitive tasks like completing purchases/payments will require verification from the end user before completion. Notably, Muse is a separate app from Instagram, Facebook or other Meta apps...and for now does not share data with META's ad system. We leave this announcement with 4 key takeaways and factors to monitor from here for META, GOOGL and the downstream players.
1) The Battle for the $30tn Consumer Agentic TAM is Heating Up: In The Morgan Stanley AI Guidebook: Navigating the Age of Inference we laid out the $30tn TAM of consumer spend (See Exhibit 1 ) that we see consumer agentic offerings addressing across digital ads, e-commerce, online travel, autonomous driving, restaurant delivery, logistics, and wearables. There are a variety of horizontal agent offerings available (from GOOGL's Gemini to increasingly capable agents from frontier labs to newly emerging startups like Instinct)...now add META's Muse to the list. As such, we direct investors to our work in Agentic Shoppers Are Coming… Who Could Win or Lose? where we detailed our views on the leading top-of-funnel platforms (including META and GOOGL) and frameworks for implications for downstream players across e-commerce, travel, rideshare, etc.
2) We See Scaled Distribution and Unique, Large Consumer Datasets as Being Critical to Personalized Agentic Offerings and Adoption: We continue to believe leading tech platforms with broad-based reach and unique data sets (like META and GOOGL) are in advantaged positions to create unique consumer agentic offerings that drive utility and garner scaled adoption. This is part of META's advantage and opportunity as we will be highly focused on how consumer adoption and integration of Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Whatsapp data into Muse Agent ramp over time. This combined with the integration of other monetizable apps and personalized datasets (including Gmail) could give Meta an edge to create a more personalized agent with new monetizable behaviors. The entry price of the product…free…is also a notable advantage that comes with scale. And we would expect META's new model (Watermelon, expected this Fall) to only make the product better.
