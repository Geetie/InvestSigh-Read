Deep|TikTok 1Q26 Update: No Visible Impact on META or GOOG Yet
APR 27, 2026 ∙ PAID
Share
We checked in with four advertising agencies this quarter to assess whether TikTok’s recent product momentum is starting to siphon budget away from META and GOOG.
So far, we have not found evidence of a meaningful impact on either META or GOOG. Last year, our agency contacts broadly expected TikTok to take share from META and GOOG ad budgets in 2026. The current view is that all three platforms are now growing in parallel.
North America Agency Expert A
1. TikTok ad budget trajectory
a. As of June 2025: 1Q25 +12% YoY, 2Q25 +24% YoY, 3Q25 forecast -15% YoY, 4Q25 forecast flat YoY.
b. As of October 2025: 3Q25 -16% YoY, 4Q25 forecast -5% YoY.
c. As of January 2026: 4Q25 flat 0% YoY, with the underlying monthly cadence of October 2025 -7%, November 2025 0%, December 2025 +6%, a sharp improvement. 1Q26 forecast +27% YoY, 2Q26 forecast +39% YoY, full-year 2026 expected to exceed +40%.
d. As of April 2026: 1Q26 +33% YoY, 2Q26 forecast +53% YoY, 3Q26 forecast +45% YoY, 4Q26 forecast +35% YoY. Full-year 2026 should come in well above the previous +40% expectation.
2. TikTok ban
a. “Last time we spoke, we thought the ban could be lifted but it wasn’t guaranteed. As you may recall, all of last year was a cycle of ‘about to be banned, then delayed, about to be banned, then delayed.’ That’s why a lot of advertisers weren’t taking TikTok

seriously, since they were waiting to see whether the ban would actually be lifted. Once it was lifted, around the end of January, a meaningful number of advertisers said, ‘OK, TikTok is now safe; if I had pulled out, I need to come back,’ or they needed to start treating it more seriously by testing new ad products. So we essentially saw a surge in interest. That obviously helped 1Q a bit, but as you can see, the bigger upward revision came in 2Q because by then you have more advertisers and a full quarter of spend.”
3. TikTok’s impact on META and GOOG
a. There may be some impact, but it is hard to identify in the numbers. As we just discussed, the numbers look very strong. For Google, the relevant comparable would be YouTube.
b. Snapchat has clearly taken a measurable hit from TikTok. We think this is partly because Snapchat is simply a smaller platform and is more concentrated in the same demographic. The fact that we can measure the impact on Snapchat probably means Meta and YouTube are also seeing some pressure.
c. As I have said before, I never expected this to be more than a 1-percentage-point headwind to Meta or YouTube. Even if it is, it is hard to disaggregate. I have not heard a single advertiser say, “I’m spending more on TikTok, so I’ll spend less on Meta or YouTube.” That is generally not how it plays out. What you see instead is that whenever budgets refresh, more dollars flow toward TikTok.
d. The clearest loser is Snapchat, and I think Snapchat got hit twice. First, Snapchat was probably over-earning last year because many TikTok advertisers used Snapchat as part of their TikTok-ban contingency plan. Second, when TikTok surged, many advertisers put Snapchat on autopilot, saying they did not have time to think about it because they were focused on TikTok. So Snapchat took a hit. Meta and YouTube were probably also affected, but it is hard to measure.
North America Agency Expert B
1. TikTok ad budget trajectory
a. As of June 2025: 1Q25 +20% YoY, 2Q25 +9% YoY, 3Q25 forecast +10% YoY, 4Q25 forecast +10% YoY.

b. As of October 2025: 3Q25 +11% YoY, 4Q25 forecast +12 to 13% YoY.
c. As of January 2026: 4Q25 +14% YoY, 1Q26 forecast +12% YoY, with acceleration in 2H. Full-year 2026 forecast +16 to 18% YoY.
d. As of April 2026: 1Q26 +15% YoY, 2Q26 forecast +17% YoY, full-year 2026 likely +18 to 20%.
e. The growth is being driven not only by product improvements but also by TikTok’s expanding ad-product supply.
2. TikTok is now offering premium inventory
a. If you have heard of TikTok Pulse, you can buy on TikTok in a non-auction format, similar to how you would pre-buy TV inventory.
b. “Hey TikTok, I sell cosmetics, I want to buy the top five TikTok channels related to people doing makeup, or some equivalent inventory.” They have all the permutations: TikTok Lineup, TikTok Pulse, available for advertisers to purchase upfront. As a result, they are pulling in significant brand budgets, whereas they were historically known for mid-funnel and lower-funnel spend (conversion, consideration, viral lift). Now they can capture sizable brand budgets through TikTok Pulse. This has been working well, and Pulse inventory has been selling out frequently. The fact that Pulse is sold out tells you there is real demand for this kind of inventory.
3. TikTok’s impact on META and GOOG
a. Yes, absolutely. As I said, TikTok will compete head-to-head with Meta. I think it competes more with Meta than with Google because of how the budgets are categorized.
b. Google is search, Google is AI, Google through YouTube is TV. These are completely different experiences. Google has a massive data advantage as a single ecosystem, and provides analytics tools and everything else to advertisers.
Fundamentally, Google is too powerful, which is why they have antitrust issues; they own the entire value chain.
c. Meta is executing well and has solid growth momentum this year, but TikTok is a real competitor because they are launching different products, and as I noted, we are starting to see TikTok poach sales talent from Meta to sell for them. So they are going after Meta’s budget. Yes, this is a threat to Meta.

d. That said, the overall pie is still growing. More budget is flowing into social media because it works for many brands. So they will keep growing together rather than fight over share. The smaller social budgets are either stagnant or losing growth. Platforms like Pinterest or Snapchat will have an even harder time competing given the pace of product updates from Meta and TikTok.
Europe Agency Expert
1. TikTok ad budget trajectory
a. As of April 2026: 1Q26 +8% YoY, 2Q26 forecast +12% YoY, accelerating to \~20% YoY by year-end 2026.
b. Full-year 2026: +20 to 23% YoY.
c. The start of the year has been slow on overall numbers and consumer confidence, but I expect TikTok to be a big winner for the full year. The +20% number could prove fairly conservative for 2026.
2. TikTok’s impact on META and GOOG
a. Maybe two years ago TikTok was taking dollars from Meta and cannibalizing the social budget. I would say the two are now growing in parallel.
Asia-Pacific Agency Expert
1. TikTok ad budget trajectory
a. As of July 2025: 1Q25 -22.7% YoY, 2Q25 -9% YoY, 3Q25 forecast +11% YoY, 4Q25 forecast +11% YoY.
b. As of October 2025: 3Q25 +9% YoY, 4Q25 forecast -5.1% YoY.
c. As of January 2026: 4Q25 -10.2% YoY, with advertisers concerned about policy and algorithm changes leading to budget migration. 2026 full year forecast TikTok -3.5%

YoY; the YoY decline is narrowing but a gap remains.
d. As of April 2026: 1Q26 -9.7% YoY, 2Q26 forecast -7.2% YoY, 2H26 turning positive, full-year -3.5% YoY.
2. TikTok has rolled out a number of valuable features recently
a. GMV Max. Smart, one-stop campaign management, including the bidding capability uplift in GMV Max. That said, we do not see this set of capabilities as having the power to take budget from Google or Meta. Within TikTok, it leverages AI to automatically identify the traffic and audience tags best suited to drive a given advertiser’s growth, with the system autonomously indexing and tracking audience tags. It can also automatically match creator inventory, identifying the right creators to deliver against the GMV Max objective across the broader resource and product matrix.
b. Shopping Ads. Launched last year, it provides a single-pane e-commerce solution that integrates video-shopping ads, livestream ads, and catalog ads, automating the full content-to-conversion path and providing some lift to overall ROI.
c. Seedance. Has provided some offset to the YoY decline in our overall TikTok revenue.
d. Beyond product, this year a number of our apparel clients are in their cross-border push phase, with strong growth, which has flowed through to incremental TikTok budgets.
e. Another factor is TikTok’s data decision system. The full data toolkit has materially improved our ability to develop user personas and run trend forecasting.
f. That said, when benchmarked against competing products such as Google’s Performance Max or Meta’s audience-persona analytics and deep tracking capabilities, TikTok remains in a relatively weaker position overall.
