At Apple’s annual developer conference next month, the
star of the show will be a series of long-delayed artiﬁcial
intelligence upgrades to the iPhone. But the company is also
expected to emphasize what could be an underrated asset in
its eﬀorts to catch up in AI: Its ability to run AI models on the
billions of Apple devices in circulation.
在下个⽉的苹果年度开发者⼤会上，iPhone 将迎来⼀系列⻓期延
迟的⼈⼯智能升级，这将成为⼤会的焦点。但该公司预计也会强
调其在追赶 AI 浪潮中可能被低估的⼀项优势：在流通中的数⼗亿
台苹果设备上运⾏ AI 模型的能⼒。
People familiar with the company’s plans for its Worldwide
Developers Conference say that Apple is likely to showcase
how the company’s 15 years of experience of designing
custom silicon chips for iPhones, Watches and Macs will give
it an advantage when running AI models locally on those
devices. Typically, AI models are run in expensive data
centers ﬁlled with powerful AI chips.
据知情⼈⼠透露，苹果在全球开发者⼤会上的计划很可能会展
示，该公司为 iPhone、Apple Watch 和 Mac 设计定制硅芯⽚的
15 年经验，将如何使其在本地设备上运⾏ AI 模型时占据优势。
通常情况下，AI 模型是在配备强⼤ AI 芯⽚的昂贵数据中⼼中运
⾏的。
Many AI queries from Apple’s devices will still have to be
processed in the cloud because of their complexity and need
for access to troves of online information. For example, as
part of an Apple agreement with Google, some user queries
to a new version of Siri will run in Google Cloud on a
licensed version of the search giant’s Gemini model. Apple
recently approved the use of a privacy technology from
Nvidia in that setting, suggesting it will use Nvidia AI chips
for at least some of its computing needs in Google Cloud,
according to people familiar with the matter.
苹果设备上的许多 AI 查询仍将不得不在云端处理，因为这些查询
的复杂性以及需要访问海量在线信息。例如，根据苹果与⾕歌的
协议，新版 Siri 的部分⽤户查询将在⾕歌云上运⾏，使⽤的是⾕
歌搜索巨头 Gemini 模型的授权版本。据知情⼈⼠透露，苹果最
近批准在该场景中使⽤英伟达的⼀项隐私技术，这表明它将在⾕
歌云中⾄少部分使⽤英伟达 AI 芯⽚来满⾜其计算需求。
But running models locally could reduce the risk of exposing
consumers’ data and prevent advertising companies from
monetizing their personal information. It could save
business customers money by reducing their consumption of
“tokens”—the units of text that cloud AI models base their
pricing on. And for Apple, pushing more AI chores onto
devices could allow it to continue to avoid the eye-watering
investments its tech peers have made in data centers.
但在本地运⾏模型可以降低消费者数据泄露的⻛险，并防⽌⼴告
公司利⽤其个⼈信息牟利。它还能通过减少企业对"令牌"（即云
端 AI 模型定价所依据的⽂本单位）的消耗来节省成本。对苹果⽽
⾔，将更多 AI 任务转移到设备端，可以使其避免像其他科技同⾏
那样在数据中⼼上投⼊巨额资⾦。
As part of its Google agreement, Apple is using a version of
Google’s large Gemini model to train a smaller version of the
model that can run locally on Apple devices, a process
known as distillation, said people familiar with the
eﬀort. Apple is also on the lookout to acquire smaller
companies that can assist in the eﬀort of shrinking down AI
models to run on its devices, people familiar with the
company said. One such company it has considered
acquiring is Liquid AI, a Cambridge, Mass.-based startup
specializing in running AI locally on devices, said people
familiar with Apple’s strategy.
据知情⼈⼠透露，作为与⾕歌合作的⼀部分，苹果正在使⽤⾕歌
⼤型 Gemini 模型的⼀个版本，通过⼀种称为"蒸馏"的技术，训
练出能在苹果设备本地运⾏的更⼩模型版本。知情⼈⼠还表示，
苹果正在物⾊能够协助其缩⼩ AI 模型以适配设备运⾏的⼩型收购
标的。据熟悉苹果战略的⼈⼠称，其考虑收购的公司之⼀是总部
位于⻢萨诸塞州剑桥市的初创公司 Liquid AI，该公司专注于在
设备端本地运⾏ AI 技术。

Apple ﬁrst began touting the privacy beneﬁts of running
models on devices in 2024, when it introduced Apple
Intelligence, a collection of new AI features it announced at
the time. Since then, it has largely been quiet on the topic,
after an embarrassing series of stumbles, including a tepid
response to its new AI features and a delay with the launch
of the new Siri.
苹果公司于 2024 年⾸次强调在设备端运⾏ AI 模型的隐私优势，
当时推出了名为"苹果智能"的⼀系列新 AI 功能。此后，由于遭遇
⼀系列令⼈尴尬的挫折——包括市场对其新 AI 功能反应冷淡以及
新版 Siri 发布延迟——该公司在该话题上基本保持沉默。
At the same time, Apple has largely sat on the sidelines as the
biggest companies in tech have poured fortunes into
building AI computing capacity in the cloud. Last year, Meta
Platforms, for example, spent $72 billion on capital
expenditures—mostly stemming from data centers—while
Microsoft spent $88 billion. Apple, meanwhile, spent only
$12.72 billion on capital expenditures during that period.
与此同时，当科技巨头们纷纷斥巨资建设云端 AI 计算能⼒时，苹
果公司却基本置身事外。例如，Meta Platforms 去年在资本⽀出
上投⼊了 720 亿美元（主要来⾃数据中⼼），微软则花费了 880
亿美元。⽽同期苹果的资本⽀出仅为 127.2 亿美元。
At times, the company’s hesitation to pour more money into
AI has prompted criticism from investors and pundits who
believe the company could risk being left behind in a future
where AI is a key ingredient in personal devices. As the tech
industry’s AI investments have swelled to mammoth
proportions—Microsoft, for one, is projecting $190 billion in
capital expenditures this year—some technologists have
started to worry about overspending on AI computing
capacity and look more favorably at Apple’s relatively
conservative bets in the category.
苹果在 AI 领域投⼊的迟疑态度，时常招致投资者和评论家的批评
——他们认为，在 AI 将成为个⼈设备关键要素的未来，苹果可能
⾯临被甩在身后的⻛险。随着科技⾏业 AI 投资规模膨胀⾄惊⼈程
度（仅微软⼀家今年就预计投⼊ 1900 亿美元资本⽀出），部分
技术专家开始担忧 AI 计算能⼒过度投资的问题，反⽽对苹果在该
领域相对保守的押注持更积极看法。
At times, the company’s hesitation to pour more money into
AI has prompted criticism from investors and pundits who
believe the company could risk being left behind in a future
where AI is a key ingredient in personal devices. As the tech
industry’s AI investments have swelled to mammoth
proportions—Microsoft, for one, is projecting $190 billion in
capital expenditures this year—some technologists have
started to worry about overspending on AI computing
capacity and look more favorably at Apple’s relatively
conservative bets in the category.
有时，苹果在 AI 投资上的犹豫不决引发了投资者和评论家的批
评，他们认为，在 AI 成为个⼈设备关键要素的未来，苹果可能⾯
临被甩在后⾯的⻛险。随着科技⾏业在 AI 领域的投资规模急剧膨
胀——例如，微软预计今年的资本⽀出将达到 1900 亿美元——
⼀些技术专家开始担忧 AI 计算能⼒上的过度投资，并对苹果在该
领域相对保守的押注持更积极的态度。
“I think the data center boom is a mistake,” said David Stout,
chief executive of Austin-based AI startup webAI.
“Intelligence is getting smaller. Data centers won’t disappear,
but the majority of work will happen at the edge. Apple
made the bet correctly there.”
“我认为数据中⼼热潮是个错误，”总部位于奥斯汀的 AI 初创公司
webAI 的⾸席执⾏官 David Stout 表示。“智能正在变得更⼩。
数据中⼼不会消失，但⼤部分⼯作将在边缘端完成。苹果在这⽅
⾯做出了正确的押注。”
Stout is among a growing cohort of AI developers who are
making bets on new businesses based on Apple hardware.
WebAI develops specialized AI applications for enterprises
that run locally on Apple chips. For example, webAI builds
tools for aviation customers with an AI trained on a massive
manual describing the intricacies of a Boeing Dreamliner
engine to assist in engine maintenance.
Stout 是越来越多基于苹果硬件押注新业务的 AI 开发者之⼀。
WebAI 为企业开发在苹果芯⽚上本地运⾏的专⽤ AI 应⽤。例
如，webAI 为航空客户构建⼯具，其 AI 基于描述波⾳梦想客机
发动机复杂细节的庞⼤⼿册进⾏训练，以协助发动机维护。
The models can run on an iPad or Mac, without the need for
an internet connection. Apple’s computers have also taken
oﬀ among techies who use them to run OpenClaw, an open-
source tool for creating AI agents that can autonomously run
a computer.
这些模型可以在 iPad 或 Mac 上运⾏，⽆需连接互联⽹。苹果的
电脑也在技术爱好者中流⾏起来，他们⽤这些设备运⾏
OpenClaw——⼀个⽤于创建能⾃主操控电脑的 AI 代理的开源⼯
具。
Technology analyst Richard Kramer of Arete Research said in
a recent note to investors that he estimates Apple has $50
billion worth of compute capacity in on-device chips “funded
by users.”
科技分析公司 Arete Research 的理查德·克莱默在最近给投资者
的⼀份报告中指出，他估计苹果在设备端芯⽚上拥有价值 500 亿
美元的计算能⼒，这些能⼒"由⽤户买单"。
Mark Suman, a former Apple senior engineering project
manager who worked on internal AI systems before leaving
in 2024, said that the billions of Apple devices around the
world collectively amount to a powerful source of AI
computing capacity.
曾在苹果内部 AI 系统团队担任⾼级⼯程项⽬经理、于 2024 年离
职的⻢克·苏曼表示，全球数⼗亿台苹果设备共同构成了强⼤的 AI
计算能⼒来源。
“Apple could deploy the largest edge-compute AI that
anybody has in the world,” said Suman, who is now co-
founder of Maple, a startup oﬀering customers an encrypted
system for accessing AI models in the cloud. “It’s really a
matter of time before they leverage that.”
"苹果可以部署全球最⼤的边缘计算 AI 系统，"苏曼说，他现在是
初创公司 Maple 的联合创始⼈，该公司为客户提供访问云端 AI
模型的加密系统。"他们利⽤这⼀优势只是时间问题。"
Apple still can’t rely entirely on on-device models for its AI
strategy. Google’s full Gemini model has trillions of
parameters—a rough measure of the complexity of an AI
model. The full model requires so much computing
horsepower that Apple has struggled to get it to work on its
own internal server infrastructure, called Private Cloud
Compute, which runs on the same Apple chips in Mac
computers, said people familiar with the situation.
苹果仍⽆法完全依赖设备端模型来⽀撑其 AI 战略。⾕歌完整的
Gemini 模型拥有数万亿参数——这是衡量 AI 模型复杂度的粗略
指标。据知情⼈⼠透露，该完整模型所需的计算能⼒极为庞⼤，
以⾄于苹果难以在其名为"私有云计算"的内部服务器基础设施上
运⾏——该设施采⽤与 Mac 电脑相同的苹果芯⽚。
Apple will likely have to take advantage of Google’s cloud
infrastructure to run parts of the new Siri, former Apple
engineers said. Still, Apple is looking for ways to run AI
services in the cloud while still oﬀering improved privacy
protections. Its decision in recent weeks to approve the use
of Nvidia’s conﬁdential compute system to handle some of
the processing of the bigger Gemini-based model inside
Google Cloud is one such eﬀort, people familiar with the
partnership said.
苹果前⼯程师表示，苹果很可能需要借助⾕歌的云基础设施来运
⾏新版 Siri 的部分功能。尽管如此，苹果仍在寻找既能通过云端
运⾏ AI 服务，⼜能提供更强隐私保护的⽅法。据知情⼈⼠透露，
苹果近⼏周决定批准使⽤英伟达的机密计算系统，来处理⾕歌云
中基于更⼤规模 Gemini 模型的部分运算，正是此类努⼒之⼀。
Conﬁdential compute is a security feature inside Nvidia
graphics processing units that encrypts data and AI models
as they are being processed. When enabled, it slightly slows
down the processing of AI queries in the cloud, but it could
help Apple keep its promises about protecting users’ privacy.
机密计算是英伟达图形处理器中的⼀项安全功能，可在数据处理
过程中对数据和 AI 模型进⾏加密。启⽤该功能会略微降低云端
AI 查询的处理速度，但有助于苹果兑现保护⽤户隐私的承诺。
