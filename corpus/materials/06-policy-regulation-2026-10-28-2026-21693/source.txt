SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The CHIPS Act (the Act) established a manufacturing incentives 
program to sustain and reestablish U.S. leadership across the 
semiconductor supply chain. To ensure that funding provided through the 
CHIPS Program does not directly or indirectly benefit foreign countries 
of concern, the Act establishes certain conditions that must be imposed 
on recipients of CHIPS funds, such as restricting engagement in any 
significant transaction involving the material expansion of 
semiconductor manufacturing capacity in foreign countries of concern 
and prohibiting certain joint research or technology licensing efforts 
with foreign entities of concern. The Department of Commerce (Commerce) 
issued a final rule to set forth terms related to these conditions and 
procedures for a covered entity to notify the Secretary of any planned 
significant transactions that may be prohibited (15 CFR 231.302). This 
information collection implements the notification procedure in the 
final rule.

II. Method of Collection

    Information will be collected electronically. Respondents submit 
information only when they plan a transaction subject to notification 
under the final rule.

collection, including the validity of the methodology and assumptions 
used; (c) Evaluate ways to enhance the quality,

utility, and clarity of the information to be collected; and (d)

    Comments that you submit in response to this notice are a matter of

at any time. While you may ask us in your comment to withhold your 
personal identifying information from public review, we cannot 
guarantee that we will be able to do so.
