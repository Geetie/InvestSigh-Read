Global Cloud Capex Tracker: Onwards & Further Upwards
Global hyperscaler capex continues to get revised higher. CY26 cash capex across the top 11 global CSPs is now$620B, w/ over $1T being spent in 2025/2026. Big 4 CSP mgmt commentary, token growth, aggregate cloud rev growth, and component supplier comments all suggest further upward revisions ahead.
Cloud capex is now tracking to ~$470B (+68% Y/Y) in 2025 ( Exhibit 2 ).
Following last week's megacap earnings reports, 2025 cash capex for the 11 largest global hyperscalers is now tracking to $469B, or 68% Y/Y growth, 4 points, or $11B, higher than entering C3Q earnings season ( Exhibit 3 ). This upward revision is being driven by positive cash capex estimate revisions at Amazon, Meta, and Alphabet, partially offset by a slight downward revision from Microsoft due to the increasing mix of capital leases within capex. At this point, capex intensity is tracking to 19.1% of revenue in 2025, up ~6 points Y/Y and a new all-time record ( Exhibit 4 ). Exhibit 4
More importantly, 2026 cloud capex is now tracking to ~$620B, or 33% Y/Y growth, 10 points, or $60B, higher than pre-3Q earnings. The uptick in 2026 capex is driven by increasingly bullish capex forward commentary by all top 4 US hyperscalers during their September-quarter earnings calls last week. And while 33% Y/Y growth in 2026 would imply a 35-point Y/Y deceleration vs. 2025, aggregate cloud capex for the top 11 spenders has been raised every quarter for, at least, the last 8 quarters. In fact, if we just go back to July 2025 (right before C2Q hyperscaler earnings), 2025-2026 aggregate Global Cloud capex was forecasted at $953B. Today, it's forecasted at $1,090B.
Point being, as monthly tokens processed grows exponentially ( Exhibit 5 ), aggregate cloud revenue for GCP/AWS/Azure accelerates ( Exhibit 7 ), data center commitments expand, and data center component suppliers highlight accelerating demand and elongating visibility, we believe there will continue to be upward pressure on hyperscaler capex estimates as global AI infrastructure spend marches towards the $3-4T per year target NVDA CEO Jensen Huang recently outlined. What specifically are the big 4 US hyperscalers saying about 2025/2026
spending plans? Big 4 hyperscaler capex continues to ramp higher, driven primarily by spending on AI infrastructure to support near-term compute shortages and longterm monetization strategies. This earnings season, management teams highlighted the need to accelerate infrastructure deployment timelines and noted that capacity constraints will sustain in the coming year. Executives across MSFT, META, AMZN and GOOGL signaled: (1) data center capacity is constrained, notably on compute and power; and (2) a significant increase and/or an acceleration of spending into 2026. All 4 US hyperscalers quantitatively/qualitatively raised its current year capex targets, and more importantly, 3 of the 4 hyperscalers talked to growing or accelerating capex spend into 2026 — GOOGL expects a "significant increase in capex" in CY26, MSFT expects capex growth to be higher in FY26 than in FY25, and META expects capex dollar growth to be "notably larger" in 2026 than 2025. While AMZN didn't comment on CY26 capex quantitatively, mgmt highlighted that power capacity for its data centers will double by 2027 from current levels, implying a strong expansion/spending plan.
