If you are still skeptical on AI investment returns within digital ads heading into Sept. 2026,
a quick look back at analyst estimates for this year’s ad revenue growth just 2 years ago was
less than half the growth rate Meta and Google Search are expected to deliver this year. In
2Q26, Meta captured nearly half of every incremental digital ad dollar. Put these two points
together, and there’s a path to Meta overtaking Google Search as the largest destination of
ad dollars before the year is out. And yet, it’s the e-commerce names that are winning.
AI is powering digital advertising growth. The AI tailwind for digital ads is multifaceted:
improving content recommendations, driving higher engagement & more online activities,
shortening the path from discovery to purchase, and enhancing ad targeting and delivery,
resulting in higher conversion and stronger ad performance. As a result, digital ad basket
has experienced a clear growth inflection over the past few years.
AI is making the big bigger. The latest few quarters showed us that AI is strengthening,
rather than disrupting, the competitive position of the largest advertising platforms. Meta,
Google and Amazon are delivering some of their strongest ad growth in years despite
their enormous scale. This likely reflects the advantages of larger platforms, including
larger data sets, easier audience targeting, better ad automation tools and more accurate
measurement capabilities, all of which enable them to deploy AI more effectively and at a
larger scale.
Meta: the biggest AI winner in digital ads? Meta arguably has seen the largest impact
from AI on ad growth and is on track to surpass Google Search this year. Excluding ‘other’
ad revenues including Maps and Gmail, we suspect Meta may already have caught up to
Google Search. Unlike Search, where users explicitly signal intent, Meta must predict what
content and ads users are most likely to engage with, making AI-driven improvements
in content recommendations, targeting, and ad measurement especially incrementally
beneficial.
But the benefits so far appear to be accruing elsewhere, namely e-commerce. The
irony of 2026 is that digital ad fundamentals have arguably never been stronger, yet digital
ad stocks have struggled, as investors have penalized Meta and Google on the other side of
the equation: unprecedented AI capex, uncertain investment returns, and questions around
long-term growth durability and AI leadership.
Meanwhile, eCommerce companies have enjoyed many of the same AI tailwinds
with far lower costs. After a brief moment stuck in the AI disrupted camp thanks to
Agentic Commerce fears, much of the digital ad performance gains ultimately direct
consumers to merchant websites more efficiently with improving conversion rates.
Revenue revisions this year for our eCom basket are in-line with our digital ad basket, yet as
eCom platforms see ROAS gains driving marketing spend efficiency and the AI disruption
risk dissipates, the eCom basket stocks have materially outperformed our digital ad basket
YTD (Exhibit 19).

AI IS POWERING A DIGITAL ADVERTISING RENAISSANCE
The AI tailwind for digital ads is multifaceted: Improving content recommendations, driving higher engagement and
more online activities; AI chatbot conversations shortening the path from discovery to purchase; enhanced ad efficacy across
targeting, delivery, and measurement, resulting in higher conversion and stronger ad performance.
• A near linear digital ad growth acceleration from post-COVID lows. Our tracked digital ad basket has experienced a
clear growth inflection over the past few years, growing 21% Y/Y for 1H26, up from ~6% in 2022 (Exhibit 1). The annual
growth rate has continued to improve sequentially with 1H26 delivering the basket’s fastest post-COVID performance
(Exhibit 2).
• AI-tailwinds driving the rare double of engagement and ad efficacy growth. Notably, it is rare to see both ad pricing
and ad impressions grow simultaneously. Typically, you see platforms drive engagement growth through global expansion
and new product launches e.g., Stories or Reels. As you ramp ad load on these surfaces, ad pricing is generally lower given
different global economics and unproven surfaces for advertisers.
• Yet AI has simultaneously powered deeper content engagement and improved ad performance, enabled this dynamic
across the industry, mostly pronounced for Meta as well as Google (Exhibit 4, Exhibit 5)
• Even smaller platforms such as Pinterest has reported positive growth in both metrics in 2Q26 (Exhibit 6), underscoring
the broad-based benefits of AI across the digital advertising ecosystem.
AI proving out economies of scale as the big get bigger: The latest few quarters showed us that AI is strengthening, rather
than disrupting, the competitive position of the largest advertising platforms. While several smaller platforms have grown into
their offering such as Reddit and AppLovin (not covered), the majority of smaller ad platforms have struggled to keep pace with
our digital ad leaders.
• Meta and Amazon the two largest share gainers. Over the past few years Meta and Amazon have dominated the share
capture of incremental digital ad dollars growing their share by 250bps combined in 2Q26 (Exhibit 9). Meta, Google and
Amazon are delivering some of their strongest ad growth in years despite their enormous scale (Exhibit 3).
• For Meta, they remain the gold standard of AI-driven performance improvements. Recall that Meta needs to (1) win over
your discretionary engagement minutes, and (2) guess which products and services you’re most interested in and when =
We discuss more below how Meta has achieved this.
• For Amazon, the growth has partially been led by creating an end-to-end digital ad solution leveraging its first party retail
media consumer data. While search has fewer AI-tailwinds than lean back media given consumer shared intent, recent
deployment and scale up of Rufus/Alexa+ have aided consumer decision-making.
• Meta and Google growing 2X+ previous Street estimates. Rewinding back the clock to mid 2024 reveals a very different
outlook on the state of digital ads. While not unusual for analysts to continuously underestimate the durability of digital
ad growth given the wrong denominator used (total advertising is not the right one, see our note: The "TAM"inator: Sizing
markets, fears and dreams - Tip of the digital advertising iceberg), it’s the magnitude of these forward revenue estimate
revisions that has been most remarkable.
• In mid 2024, consensus 2026 Google Search ad revenue growth was a cool 8% Y/Y. T oday, the Street now sits at
a growth rate 2X that stale estimate at 16% Y/Y (Exhibit 7). AI Overviews and AI Mode are expanding Search into more
conversational and complex use cases. AI Mode queries are ~3x longer than traditional searches, contributing to record
query volume and 1B+ monthly AI Mode users by 2Q26. Google's Gemini-powered ad products are improving query
understanding and ad matching, with AI-powered campaigns delivering 15%+ higher conversions at similar ROAS. While
continued uptake of PMAX/AI-MAX allow Google to further take control over ad budgets, delivery, and ad creative.
• In mid 2024, consensus 2026 Meta ad revenue growth stood at a meager 12% Y/Y. T oday, Street estimates are
2.2X the old number at 26%, with the whisper number pointing to 20%+ in 2027 (Exhibit 8).

META: COMING HARD FOR THE DIGITAL AD THRONE… THANKS TO AI
Meta is on track to surpass Google Search as the largest digital ad platform in the world by year-end. As we’ve
often noted, Meta has seen arguably the largest positive impact from AI on ad growth thanks to a rebuilt AI-powered content
recommendation algorithm, and rebuilt AI-powered ad platform to better match ads delivered to the right audience at the right
time, and most importantly get credit for the conversion events.
• Turns out we can spend way more time on Meta properties. Meta continues to improve recommendations through larger
AI models and richer content understanding, helping drive Facebook time spent +5% (in 3Q25), US Instagram Reels watch
time grow 30% Y/Y (in 4Q25), and Threads time spent +20% (in 4Q25).
• On ad performance, Meta's AI investments in ranking, targeting, automation and generative creative tools continue to gain
traction, with Advantage+ reaching a $75B annual revenue run-rate by 2Q26. There’s a long series of published whitepapers from Meta covering a whole host of AI-powered improvements including GEM, Lattice, and Andromeda. It’s important
to note that many of the improvements to-date aren’t fully LLM-powered yet, and AI ad creative efforts are just getting
underway = we believe there’s a multi-year runway ahead for continued AI-fueled ad performance enhancements.
• As AI drives engagement, online activity and advertising effectiveness, both Meta and Google appear well positioned to
sustain healthy digital advertising growth. Meta management has repeatedly emphasized confidence in the durability of
these trends and visibility into multi-year growth opportunities (Exhibit 16).

Search soon is baked into Consensus (Exhibit 10, Exhibit 11). It is worth noting that Meta and pure Google Search excluding
Maps, Gmail, etc. are likely already neck and neck.

INVESTOR BENEFITS HAVE LOGICALLY ACCRUED ELSEWHERE, NAMELY E-COMMERCE
The irony of 2026 is that digital ad fundamentals have arguably never been stronger, yet digital ad stocks have
struggled.
• The burden of AI investment - what could, should, would. Perhaps without the overhangs of the scale of AI infrastructure
investment and on-again/off-again debates around AI competitive positioning, META and GOOGL could be easy consensus
longs during this AI-trade. GOOGL certainly had an exceptional 2025 primarily tied to the durability of strong Google Search
ad performance for the reasons we touched on above, but also aided by strong performance in Google Cloud and Gemini
model development.
• AI digital ads tailwinds unsurprisingly aid eCom platforms too. The digital ad basket continues to sustain high growth,
and have seen revenue estimates revised upward at a pace comparable to eCommerce basket (Exhibit 18). It stands to
reason that if consumers are spending more time online and finding their desired products quicker, then ad performance
improves proportionately driving higher sales volume (Note: eCom Basket includes AMZN, SHOP, W, EBAY, ETSY).
