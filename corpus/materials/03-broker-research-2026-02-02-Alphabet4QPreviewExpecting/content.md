4Q Preview: Expecting an upside quarter
on usage ramp
Reiterate Rating: BUY | PO: 370.00 USD | Price: 338.25 USD
Expect 4Q revenue/EPS beat, slightly raising estimates
Reports 2/4. Strong Meta results suggest a sable 4Q ad market, and we think usage may have accelerated on Gemini 3 release, we are slightly raising search estimates (to 15% growth vs 14%). We think Search growth expectations could be 15-16% (vs Street at 13%) and YouTube 14-15% (vs 13%). We now expect 4Q Rev/EPS of $95.9bn/$2.65 vs Street $95.2bn/$2.64. We est. 4Q Opex of $28.0bn (+13% y/y) with operating margins +119bps y/y. Job data suggests disciplined hiring trends with openings down 7% q/q (vs Street +0.5% q/q). We think search upside can flow though to EPS, and other income should be positive, though 1x charges/accruals can surprise. Reiterate Buy.
1Q comments key for stock, raising 2026 capex estimates
Sentiment is positive, plus Meta has set a high bar for 1Q, and we think strong Gemini traffic data points, optimism on 1Q ad growth, commentary suggesting AI is aiding search monetization, enthusiasm on new products (Agentic), and strong Cloud results & backlog is needed (and likely) on the call to support stock. For 1Q, we now est. Rev/EPS of $90.1bn/$2.56 vs Street $88.8bn/$2.54. Given Meta spend, we are raising FY26 capex by 14% to $139bn & expect guide, if offered, around $140bn (vs Street at $119bn).
Potential positives and risks for 4Q call
4Q positives could include: Search upside from accelerating usage, data suggesting strong Gemini app & AI mode traction, improving GCP trends vs Azure (Gemini 3.0 & TPU traction), and AI-driven efficiency gains driving R&D leverage. Risks: Inline/miss on Search vs high expectations, less ad upside relative to Meta, commentary suggesting possible future margin headwinds on capacity build, and 1x cost surprises.
Constructive on 2026 Search and Cloud upside potential
With a growing percentage of Google searches served in AI-native formats, we think Street could be underestimating Search upside in 2026 driven by higher engagement, improved conversion rates, and incremental monetization opportunities. For Cloud, we believe Gemini 3.0 and TPUs are increasingly differentiating Google offerings, which could support new mega-deal wins. Risk is elevated valuation (P/E now above MSFT) and new competitive product launches (LLMs, OpenAI ad ramp) could weigh on relative AI sentiment. For detailed bull & bear views, see our 2026 Alphabet PM Level Outlook.

Company Description
Alphabet is a global technology company focused around key areas, such as search, advertising, operating systems and platforms, enterprise and hardware products. The company generates revenue primarily by delivering online advertising and by selling apps and contents on Google Play as well as hardware products. The company provides its products and services in more than 100 languages and in 190 countries, regions, and territories.
Investment Rationale
We see Alphabet as well positioned long term with leading AI technology to apply to search, YouTube and Cloud businesses. Alphabet should also benefit from increasing mobile usage, video usage, Google Play activity, and connected device activity (including autos). We believe that Alphabet should trade at a premium to a media peer group given technology leadership, high margins, and strong cash flow generation for buybacks.

Estimate Changes
We are raising our advertising revenues as Meta’s results and checks/data suggest a healthy 4Q holiday ad spending, plus FX has been favorable. In addition, we are updating Other Income to reflect a potential mark-to-market benefit from Alphabet’s stake in public and private companies such as SpaceX and Anthropic. For 4Q’25, we are raising Search and YouTube revenues by 1% to $61.9bn (+15% y/y) and $12.0bn (+15% y/y) respectively. Our 4Q Cloud revenue estimates remain unchanged. We are raising Other Income to $1.3bn (from $0.8bn) and now estimate total net revenue/EPS of $95.9bn/$2.65 (vs $95.3bn/$2.58). For 2026, we are raising net revenue by 1% to $400bn, Opex by 0.4% to $117bn and GAAP EPS by 1% to $11.22. We also view Meta’s significantly higher-than-expected capex guidance as indicative of sustained, elevated sector-wide infrastructure investment. We are revising Alphabet’s 2026 capex upward by 14% to $139bn (+50% y/y) and lowering FCF to $53bn (down 13% y/y).

4Q’25 Preview Summary
4Q expectations (post Meta and Microsoft results)
Alphabet is scheduled to report 4Q results on Feb 4, after market close. Meta/MSFT results suggest a stable 4Q for ad & cloud spend, with potential ad acceleration in 1Q. Cloud capacity seems to be key driver of Cloud revenues (for now), and Google has been accelerating capacity growth. We think Street expectations into results could be 15-16% y/y growth for Search (vs Street at 13%), 14-15% for YouTube (vs 13%), and 36-37% for Cloud (vs 35%). Potential revenue upside, and a positive market environment for equity investments are also expected to drive EPS upside. We think relative performance vs META/MSFT, and 1Q commentary will drive stock performance. Biggest potential to drive upside and multiple expansion in 2026, in our view, is AI driven benefits to search monetization, and optimism on Agentic AI rollout. We think Cloud could beat as well.
4Q BofA estimates vs Street
For Search, we now estimate 4Q revenues of $61.9bn (vs Street at $61.2bn), with growth stable q/q at 15% y/y on a 1pt tougher y/y comp. For YouTube, we now estimate revenue of $12.0bn (vs Street $11.8bn), with growth stable q/q at 15% on a 2pt tougher y/y comp. For Cloud, we estimate revenue of $16.2bn (vs Street $16.3bn), with growth accelerating 1pt q/q to 35% on a 5pt easier y/y comp.
Checks suggest ad innovation improving at Google
Meta results suggests a stable 4Q ad market and eCommerce strength. Management noted 4Q ad revenue upside (vs guidance) was driven by strong holiday demand, usage acceleration and AI-driven performance gains. Our channel checks suggest a modest q/q acceleration in search growth in 4Q. They noted that Google is gaining momentum in advertising innovation, supported by broader integration of Gemini across its ecosystem. Search is benefiting from AI driven products such as Performance Max and AI Max, which better utilize user data across the Google ecosystem for targeting and attribution. Checks also suggested that Gemini integrations are not only enhancing the core search experience but are also improving the broader advertising stack, including higher quality automated video creation for YouTube ads. Industry data suggests that while AI search formats may be impacting overall query and click volumes, they are driving higher conversion rates, which could more than offset any traffic pressure. Also, the quality of engagement has improved, as consumers increasingly use AI tools to refine decisions before clicking, which is boosting Search conversions and supporting higher CPCs.
Our checks on YouTube suggest growth remains healthy and potential for stable to slight acceleration in 4Q y/y growth, primarily driven by increased performance spend. They suggest Google’s expanding Gen-AI creative toolkit is lowering barriers to video advertising by simplifying asset creation and campaign execution. Incremental advertisers on YouTube are primarily SMBs using Performance Max, which automatically generates video assets, deploys them across YouTube, and incorporates performanceoriented formats such as QR codes. They also suggest that YouTube is benefiting from shared data enhancements across broader Google ads ecosystem, improving targeting & ROAS and ramping Shorts monetization remains an incremental growth driver.
