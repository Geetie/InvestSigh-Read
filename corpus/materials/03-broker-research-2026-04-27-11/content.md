AMZN and META: A Graviton Enabled Muse Ahead?
AMZN and META's expanded Graviton agreement re-enforces our bullish AWS growth and margin outlook. It could add 1-3 points of growth to AWS in '27. It also signals META is laying the groundwork for more Muse enabled agentic offerings ahead...a potential multi-year revenue and multiple expander.
AMZN and META Expand CPU and Graviton Agreement as AI Driven CPU Demand Accelerates… AMZN and META have signed a new agreement whereby META will get access to "10s of millions" of Graviton cores (Graviton5 has 192 cores/chip)...with the potential to expand as META's AI capabilities and agentic products and services grow.
...More Positive Signal for AWS's Rapidly-Growing, High Margin Graviton Business…As written in The Graviton and Axion Bull Cases for AMZN and GOOGL, we see the rise of agentic AI workloads unlocking significant TAM upside for the CPU market and AMZN's custom CPU offering (Graviton). This agreement provides further evidence of this thesis and showcases how AMZN is well positioned to drive and benefit from this AI CPU opportunity. We estimate Graviton is already a ~$6-7bn run rate business growing ~50% y/y (making a tailwind to near-term and multi-year AWS growth), carries a higher incremental margin (~twice the gross margin of AI workloads) and is a further driver of AWS differentiation, cost leadership and customer stickiness. Further, we estimate this agreement with META can add another ~$2-$5bn to Graviton's ARR (depending on interpretation of tens of millions) and potentially contribute 1-3ppts to '27 AWS growth (assuming fully ramped by year-end '26), see Exhibit 1 . Please let us know if interested in our sensitivity analysis.
