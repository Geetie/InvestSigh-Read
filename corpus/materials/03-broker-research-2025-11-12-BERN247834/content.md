Pop Mart: High Frequency Data Tracker - Monitoring 20+ leading indicators as global momentum moderates
Following our Pop Mart Initiation on October 16th, we launch this Pop Mart High Frequency Data Tracker, monitoring 20+ data points and sources across its key IPs. Multiple independent data sources indicate broad-based demand deterioration in both China and overseas markets during October, with weakness intensifying from peak levels in June. The convergence of weakening transaction data, social media engagement, and search interest across independent sources paints a picture of fundamental demand deceleration difficult to dismiss as noise or channel shifts.
China transaction data shows sharp deceleration across channels. Offline transactions via Alipay slowed to 54% YoY growth in October, slightly below Q3. Online GMV for both ecommerce platforms and Pop Mart's flagship stores fell to the lowest level in 16 months (Moojing), potentially indicating Q4 revenue deceleration.
Social media and search engagement collapsed uniformly across platforms. Red Note interactions for Labubu declined 86-91% MoM (Qiangua), while Baidu and WeChat indices fell 14-27% and 19% respectively in October.
The secondary market shows modest prices decline but transaction volumes collapse. Average resale prices on Qiandao declined 1% MoM and 49% from June, while transaction volumes fell 38% MoM and 76% from June. While increased production by Pop Mart and greater availability in official stores may be contributing to price pressure, the collapse in transaction volumes could indicate sellers are holding substantial inventory and unable to find buyers even at lower levels. The 76% volume decline from June signaling the secondary market for Labubu has lost momentum. Non-secret SKUs now carry only 12% premiums or trade at discounts for some series.
Overseas website and app traffic deteriorated, mirroring China trends. Similarweb shows worldwide visits to popmart.com fell 26% MoM with YoY growth decelerating sharply from 736% in September to 147% in October. App downloads falling 57% MoM and 88% from June, while DAU declined 24% MoM (Sensor Tower). [Continued to next page]
