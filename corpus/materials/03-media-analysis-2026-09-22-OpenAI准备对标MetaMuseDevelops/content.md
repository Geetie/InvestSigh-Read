OpenAI pioneered AI that can take over web browsers
and other applications on people’s behalf, and its ChatGPT
app brought generative AI to consumers. Now that rivals
like SpaceX and Meta are pushing the boundaries by
launching personal AI agents that handle multistep tasks,
OpenAI is playing catch-up.
OpenAI 率先开发出能够代表⽤户接管⽹⻚浏览器及其他
应⽤程序的 AI，其 ChatGPT 应⽤也将⽣成式 AI 带给了
消费者。如今，随着 SpaceX 和 Meta 等竞争对⼿通过推
出能够处理多步骤任务的个⼈ AI 代理不断拓展边界，
OpenAI 正努⼒迎头赶上。
For instance, OpenAI is developing features to compete
more directly with SpaceX’s recently launched Grok Bot,
which helps customers create always-on AI “teammates” to
handle routine tasks, manage software, and collaborate in
the background, according to a person with knowledge of
the eﬀort. OpenAI also has discussed developing a personal
AI assistant to compete with one that Meta Platforms
recently launched, according to another person with
knowledge of the situation.
据⼀位知情⼈⼠透露，例如，OpenAI 正在开发相关功
能，以更直接地与 SpaceX 近期推出的 Grok Bot 展开竞
争。Grok Bot 可帮助客户创建始终在线的 AI“队友”，在
后台处理⽇常任务、管理软件并开展协作。据另⼀位了解
情况的⼈⼠透露，OpenAI 还讨论过开发⼀款个⼈ AI 助
⼿，以与 Meta Platforms 近期推出的产品竞争。
In developing such products, OpenAI would be
repurposing and rebranding some of the existing “agentic”
technology it already oﬀers through Codex and ChatGPT.
Some customers of these OpenAI apps have already
connected them to their email, calendar and other
applications so they can automate tasks such as scheduling
appointments or communicating with businesses by email,
sometimes over multiple days. 
在开发此类产品时，OpenAI 将重新利⽤并重新包装其已
通过 Codex 和 ChatGPT 提供的部分“代理式”技术。
OpenAI 这些应⽤的⼀些客户已经将其连接到电⼦邮件、
⽇历和其他应⽤程序，以便⾃动执⾏安排预约或通过电⼦
邮件与企业沟通等任务，有时任务会持续数⽇。
And OpenAI has said that since GPT-5 last year, each
successive AI model has been designed to tackle longer-
horizon tasks, including in those apps.
OpenAI 表示，⾃去年推出 GPT-5 以来，其后续每⼀款
AI 模型的设计⽬标都是应对时间跨度更⻓的任务，包括
在这些应⽤中的任务。
But many OpenAI users aren’t necessarily aware of these
features, and it’s safe to say SpaceX and Meta have stolen
some thunder with their respective agent products in
recent weeks.
但许多 OpenAI ⽤户未必了解这些功能，可以肯定地说，
SpaceX 和 Meta 凭借各⾃的智能体产品在最近⼏周抢⾛
了⼀些⻛头。
Grok Bot, launched in August, aims to help people quickly
set up bots that specialize in diﬀerent tasks, such as
performing market research or developing an ad
campaign. Customers can give the bots access to their work
applications, such as Gmail and Google Calendar, Notion,
Figma, or accounting, ﬁnance, AI coding assistants or other
software so they can perform tasks within those apps. Grok
Bot customers can also tell the AI to watch them perform
certain tasks with various apps so it can learn to repeat
them on its own.
Grok Bot 于 8 ⽉推出，旨在帮助⼈们快速设置专⻔执⾏
不同任务的机器⼈，例如进⾏市场调研或制定⼴告活动。
客户可以授权这些机器⼈访问其⼯作应⽤，例如 Gmail
和 Google Calendar、Notion、Figma，或会计、⾦
融、AI 编程助⼿及其他软件，从⽽让它们在这些应⽤中
执⾏任务。Grok Bot 客户还可以让 AI 观察他们使⽤各
种应⽤执⾏特定任务，以便 AI 学会⾃主重复这些操作。
The bots can report back when they ﬁnish a task or need
input from the customer. Customers can also require the
bots to seek approval before taking actions, such as
sending an email, making a purchase or ﬁnancial transfer,
or changing data in another app.
机器⼈完成任务或需要客户提供信息时，可以向客户反
馈。客户还可以要求机器⼈在采取⾏动前先寻求批准，例
如发送电⼦邮件、进⾏购买或资⾦转账，或更改另⼀款应
⽤中的数据。

SpaceX ⼀直在将 Grok Bot 定位为⽩领和⼩企业主的某

⼀直在社交媒体、YouTube 和播客上⼤⼒宣传 Grok

Similarly, Meta this month launched Muse to automate
tasks such as communicating with businesses, answering
emails, shopping for products and other things people
might do from their phone. Meta’s move followed the early
rise of personal assistant app Instinct, which is available by
invitation only but has more than 100,000 users.
同样，Meta 本⽉推出了 Muse，⽤于⾃动完成与企业沟
通、回复电⼦邮件、购买产品以及⼈们可能通过⼿机完成
的其他任务。Meta 的这⼀举措紧随个⼈助理应⽤
Instinct 的初步崛起之后。Instinct ⽬前仅限受邀使
⽤，但⽤户数已超过 100,000。
Instinct, which launched before Muse, is trying to raise
capital at a $10 billion valuation. It gained attention
because it represented a potential breakthrough in AI for
consumers after ChatGPT growth slowed dramatically over
the past year. (The chatbot earlier this summer surpassed 1
billion weekly active users, a target OpenAI had aimed to
hit last year.)
Instinct 在 Muse 推出之前便已上线，⽬前正试图以$10
billion 的估值筹集资本。它之所以受到关注，是因为在
过去⼀年 ChatGPT 增⻓⼤幅放缓之后，Instinct 代表了
⾯向消费者的 AI 领域潜在突破。（今年夏初，这款聊天
机器⼈超越了 1 billion weekly active users，达到了
OpenAI 原本计划于去年实现的⽬标。）
While consumers have gravitated to AI chatbots like
ChatGPT and Google’s Gemini to retrieve information and
get advice or help with a variety of projects, such chatbots
haven’t had as much success in handling more complex,
personal tasks such as booking services online.
尽管消费者纷纷转向 ChatGPT 和 Google 的 Gemini 等
AI 聊天机器⼈，以获取信息并就各种项⽬寻求建议或帮
助，但这类聊天机器⼈在处理在线预订服务等更复杂、更
具个性化的任务⽅⾯，尚未取得同样的成功。
Tech entrepreneurs and venture capitalists raved online
about Instinct’s ability to complete a range of tasks of
varying complexity, including managing calendars and
ordering supplies for a wedding. Users communicate with
Instinct through messaging apps, and can give it access to
credit card information and passwords to allow the AI bot
to shop or book travel.
科技创业者和⻛险投资家在⽹上盛赞 Instinct 完成各种
不同复杂程度任务的能⼒，包括管理⽇历以及为婚礼订购
物资。⽤户通过即时通信应⽤与 Instinct 沟通，并可以
向其提供信⽤卡信息和密码，以便该 AI 机器⼈购物或预
订旅⾏服务。
It remains to be seen how many consumers are ready to

⽬前尚不清楚有多少消费者愿意让这类 AI 智能体访问个
⼈信息，从⽽使其能够执⾏有⽤的任务。
Meta has billions of users it can put Muse in front of, and in

Meta 拥有数⼗亿⽤户，可以将 Muse 推送给他们；近⼏
⽇，Meta 还在 Messenger 等应⽤中⼤⼒推⼴这款助
⼿。然⽽，Meta 的分发优势在 2024 年并未发挥理想作
⽤，当时该公司推出了⼀款旨在与 ChatGPT 竞争的聊天

It isn’t clear when OpenAI might launch features to counter

⽬前尚不清楚 OpenAI 何时可能推出针对 Grok Bot 和

What OpenAI Researchers See That We Can’t
OpenAI 研究⼈员看到⽽我们看不到的东⻄
In the debate over how scared we should be that AI may
“kill us all” one day, some have been quick to point out that
AI has yet to achieve milestones we might expect before it’s
capable of taking over the world: boosting GDP, curing
diseases, or automating a substantial number of jobs.
在关于我们应该多么担⼼ AI 有朝⼀⽇可能“杀死我们所有
⼈”的争论中，⼀些⼈很快指出，AI 尚未实现我们可能认
为其具备接管世界能⼒之前应达到的⾥程碑：推动 GDP
增⻓、治愈疾病，或实现⼤量⼯作⾃动化。
One factor that could help to explain the gap between the
public’s experience with AI and the strongly-worded
warnings from researchers in the ﬁeld is the fact that those
employees often use new models many months before they
reach the masses, as we detailed in this story this morning.
The piece focuses on previously unreported talks between
Anthropic and OpenAI around testing each others’ models
for safety.
⼀个可能有助于解释公众对 AI 的实际体验与该领域研究
⼈员措辞强烈的警告之间差距的因素是：正如我们在今天
早上的这篇报道中详细介绍的那样，这些员⼯通常会在新
模型⾯向⼤众推出前数⽉使⽤它们。该报道聚焦于此前未
曾报道的 Anthropic 与 OpenAI 之间的讨论，双⽅围绕
相互测试模型的安全性展开交流。
Researchers tell us AI has largely automated the process of
training new experimental models. Moreover, they also
said models within OpenAI today largely write the
programs needed to run or train models on graphics
processing units (otherwise known as GPU kernels) and
the optimizations for those programs. 
研究⼈员告诉我们，AI 已基本实现了新实验模型训练流
程的⾃动化。此外，他们还表示，如今 OpenAI 内部的模
型⼤多能够编写在图形处理单元（graphics processing
units，亦称 GPU kernels）上运⾏或训练模型所需的程
序，以及针对这些程序的优化代码。
Engineers can provide the model with a single example of
the type of optimization that they want and the AI can run
for weeks to implement such optimizations, one OpenAI
employee said. This level of AI-powered automation only
became possible in the last few months thanks to
improvements in models, the employee said.
⼀名 OpenAI 员⼯表示，⼯程师可以向模型提供⼀个他们
希望实现的优化类型的示例，⽽ AI 则能运⾏数周来实施
此类优化。该员⼯称，这种由 AI 驱动的⾃动化⽔平直到
过去⼏个⽉才成为可能，这得益于模型性能的改进。
In another example, the OpenAI employee also said that it’s
not uncommon for employees’ agents to work together to
solve problems without ever looping in their human users.
另⼀个例⼦是，这名 OpenAI 员⼯还表示，员⼯的智能体
相互协作解决问题，却始终不让其⼈类⽤户介⼊，这种情
况并不少⻅。
Sometimes that can backﬁre, though: the staﬀer said that
they had noticed times where employees would, for
instance, ask their agents to make changes to the
company’s codebase and those agents would message other
employees on Slack and ask them to ﬁx bugs in the code
the agents found, even if their user didn’t ask them to do so
or if ﬁxing the bugs weren’t relevant to their work.
(Imagine if you asked your agent to complete a task for you
and you opened Slack to see your agent chewing out your
coworkers for their lazy work!)
不过，有时这也会适得其反：该员⼯表示，他们曾注意到
这样的情况——例如，员⼯让⾃⼰的智能体修改公司的代
码库，⽽这些智能体会在 Slack 上联系其他员⼯，要求他
们修复智能体发现的代码漏洞，即使⽤户并未要求智能体
这样做，或者修复这些漏洞与相关员⼯的⼯作并⽆关系。
（想象⼀下：你让智能体替你完成⼀项任务，结果打开
Slack，却发现你的智能体正在斥责同事⼯作懒散！）
A lot of these concerns have to do with reward hacking, or
the idea that an AI might ﬁnd loopholes or take unexpected
steps to complete a goal its user gives it. That’s diﬀerent
from an AI acting maliciously on purpose or a bad actor
using AI to commit a crime.
许多这类担忧都与“奖励劫持”（reward hacking）有
关，即 AI 可能会找到漏洞，或采取意料之外的步骤，以
完成⽤户赋予它的⽬标。这不同于 AI 蓄意实施恶意⾏
为，也不同于不法分⼦利⽤ AI 犯罪。
OpenAI has taken steps to prevent this type of behavior, we
reported. You could imagine, for instance, researchers
adding to an AI’s prompt instructions telling it not to
extrapolate beyond the exact instructions its user gives it.
That’s a careful balancing act, however, because you can
also imagine that users might not want to have to write out
every single step it wants an agent to take.
正如我们报道的那样，OpenAI 已采取措施防⽌此类⾏
为。例如，你可以设想，研究⼈员会在 AI 的提示词中加
⼊指令，要求它不要超出⽤户明确给出的指令进⾏推断。
不过，这需要谨慎权衡，因为你也可以设想，⽤户可能不
希望必须逐⼀写出希望代理执⾏的每个步骤。
There are signs that the rate of AI improvements could
accelerate. One reason is OpenAI’s increased access to
servers. The ChatGPT-maker’s research team has developed
numerous techniques or tricks they have wanted to
implement in new models but didn't previously have
enough servers to test those theories quickly. Now they do,
and what might have previously taken years to pull oﬀ
now happens in just a week, employees say. 
有迹象表明，AI 改进的速度可能会加快。其中⼀个原因
是 OpenAI 能够获得更多服务器资源。ChatGPT 的开发
商的研究团队已经开发出许多他们希望应⽤于新模型的技
术或诀窍，但此前没有⾜够的服务器来快速测试这些理
论。员⼯表示，现在他们有了⾜够的资源，过去可能需要
数年才能实现的事情，如今⼀周内就能完成。
Some of these ideas for improving models are more than a
year old, one of the people said. That includes recurrent
depth or looping techniques in training and running
advanced models.—Stephanie Palazzolo and Amir Efrati
其中⼀些改进模型的想法已有⼀年多历史，其中⼀位⼈⼠
表示。这包括在训练和运⾏⾼级模型时采⽤循环深度或循
环技术。——Stephanie Palazzolo 和 Amir Efrati
