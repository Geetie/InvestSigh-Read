Meta is training the OpenClaw-inspired agent, internally called “Hatch,” with the goal of internal testing by the end of June, one of the people said. The aim is for Hatch, the name of which might change once it is launched, to be able to perform a range of tasks for people. As part of its training work, for example, Meta has built “sandboxed” web environments where the agent can be tested on simulations of real websites such as DoorDash, Etsy, Reddit, Yelp, and Outlook, the person said.
Meta 正在训练一款受 OpenClaw 启发的内部名为“哈奇（” Hatch）的智能体，其中一位知情人士表示，该公司目标是在6月底前完成内部测试。哈奇的名称在正式推出后可能会更改，其研发目的是让它能够为人类完成一系列任务。该人士透露，作为训练工作的一部分，Meta 已搭建“沙盒”式网络环境，可在该环境中让智能体在 DoorDash、Etsy、Reddit、Yelp 以及 Outlook 等真实网站的模拟场景下进行测试。

Meta’s goal is “to deliver agents that can understand your goals and then work day and night to help you achieve them,” he said last week on the company’s quarterly earnings call, when the company also said it was increasing its capital spending on AI infrastructure this year to as much as \$145 billion.

He also described on that call some of the technical challenges to making an OpenClawlike tool work for Meta’s billions of users, requiring extensive infrastructure as well as smooth and easy use. 他还在那次通话中介绍了打造一款类似 OpenClaw 的工具以服务 Meta 数十亿用户所面临的一些技术挑战，这需要完善的基础设施，同时还得保证操作便捷流畅。OpenClaw 今年在科技爱好者中迅速走红，他们用这款工具打造自 己的自主智能体，但对于大多数非技术用户来说，它实在过于复杂了。

Meta 今年早些时候曾试图收购 OpenClaw，该软件的创作者彼得·施泰因贝格尔在一档播客中透露。但 OpenAI 已于 2 月聘请了施泰因贝格尔，他表示自 己正着手成立一个基金会来管理这款软件。

Meta is ramping up efforts to improve Hatch’s ability to decide when to take initiative rather than wait for instructions, the person familiar with Hatch said. The company is also expanding the amount of information the model can process at one time and improving its memory so it can retain details across conversations. At the same time, Meta is refining how the agent responds to users and how it selects and uses tools.
这位熟悉 Hatch 的人士表示，Meta 正在加大力度，提升 Hatch 主动采取行动而非等待指令的能力。该公司还在扩充模型单次可处理的信息量，并优化其记忆功能，以便它能在多轮对话中保留细节。与此同时，Meta 正在完善智能体对用户的回应方式，以及它选择和使用工具的逻辑。

The idea of shopping agents has been especially buzzy among big tech companies. Google in January launched Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience, a platform that includes an AI-powered shopping agent capable of recommending products, building carts, and completing purchases with user approval. Amazon also offers an AI shopping assistant, Rufus, which helps customers track prices, research and buy products.
购物助手的概念在大型科技公司中尤为热门。谷歌于1月推出了面向客户体验的GeminiEnterprise平台，其中包含一款人工智能购物助手，能够推荐商品、帮用户添加购物车并在获得用户授权后完成购买。亚马逊也推出了人工智能购物助手Rufus，可帮助顾客查询价格、调研商品并完成购买。
With the integration with Instagram, Meta hopes its agentic shopping tool can increase competition with TikTok’s e-commerce feature, TikTok Shop, according to a fourth person familiar with the plans.
据第四位知情人士透露，通过与Instagram的整合，Meta希望其智能购物工具能增强与TikTok电商功能TikTok Shop的竞争。
Hatch users will be able to tap on an item in an Instagram Reel or feed to learn more about it, navigate to an external webpage and complete a purchase within the platform, the person said.
这位人士表示，Hatch 用户将可以点击 Instagram Reel 或动态中的商品以了解更多信息，还能跳转至外部网页并在平台内完成购买。
The tool will build on new AI-powered shopping experiences that Meta announced in March, which included features that surface more detailed product information with AI and a new checkout experience that lets users make a purchase by just clicking an ad.
该工具将以 Meta 于 3 月份宣布的全新人工智能驱动购物体验为基础打造，其中包含利用人工智能展示更详细产品信息的功能，以及一种全新的结账体验，用户只需点击广告即可完成购买。
Shopping agents face a number of hurdles, including resistance from some platforms to allowing other companies’ agents. CEO Andy Jassy said last week that the experience with third-party agents on the platform “hasn’t gotten great” yet, noting they are “not often able to get the pricing right or the product information right.”
购物代理面临诸多障碍，包括部分平台抵制允许其他公司的代理入驻。首席执行官安迪·贾西上周表示，平台上第三方代理的使用体验“还没有变得很好”，并指出它们“往往无法准确获取价格或产品信息”。

Meta employees are also using an internal AI agent, MyClaw, for tasks such as accessing work files, summarizing posts on the company’s message forum, and getting technical advice. Use of MyClaw recently triggered a major security alert at Meta after an employee followed incorrect advice from the agent, which led to sensitive company and user data being exposed to employees who were not authorized to access it, as previously reported by The Information.
Meta 的员工也在使用内部人工智能智能体 MyClaw 处理访问工作文件、汇总公司消息论坛的帖子以及获取技术建议等任务。据《The Information》此前报道，近期一名员工采纳了该智能体给出的错误建议，导致 MyClaw 的使用触发了 Meta 的一次重大安全警报，致使公司敏感数据和用户信息泄露给了未获授权的员工。
Meta’s interest in agents led to its December acquisition of Manus, an AI agent developed by a Chinese startup. But last month, China’s National Development and Reform Commission said Meta would be required to unwind it.
Meta 对智能体的兴趣促成其在去年 12 月收购了 Manus，这是一家中国初创公司开发的人工智能智能体。但上个月，中国国家发展和改革委员会表示，Meta 被要求解除这笔收购。
