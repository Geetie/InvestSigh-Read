China AI Monthly: US vs China Valuation
Our update shows US AI valuation has risen ~US$8trn/42% since Jul 2024,
implying 35x incremental 2026E PE and driven mainly by opticals/memory.
China's AI valuation since DS (Jan 13) has risen ~US$732bn/88%, implying
253x incremental PE and driven most by SPE/foundries. We see further
upside in China, as the mkt cap of our China AI basket is only 8% of the US,
but the capex ratio is 18%. China's AI model performance gap vs the US
further shrank to 6% (from 8%).
China saw more powerful re-rating in an early stage vs the US but could have more upside.
We updated our US (=global ex China) AI valuation (Jan 5 price closes, last update Jul 2024) by
adding AI app, IDC and optical stocks (now total 46), and introduced China valuation using the
same methodology (33 stocks). Since Jul 2024, the US AI basket has risen by ~US$8trn/42%.
Since the advent of ChatGPT, it has skyrocketed by ~US$18trn/218%. Based on cons, we
estimate US AI valuation is now at 26x 2026E PE (29x ex memory). Since Jul 2024, US AI's
appreciation implies 35x incremental 2026E PE. For China, we use Jan 13 2025 as the starting
point, before DeepSeek (DS) was widely known. Since then China's AI basket has risen by US
$732bn/88%, trading at ~24x 2026E PE. Based on cons, we est. China AI's rally so far implies
253x incremental 2026E PE, which is much higher than the US given China's early stage of AI
and thus more upside in earnings. We see upside to China AI's mkt cap since China as a %
of the US in market value is only 6.5% (8% if we include major unlisted co.s based on private
market valuation), but China's AI capex (2023-25E) is 18% of the US and likely rising.
Subsectors' valuation: CSP/AI apps/IDC lower in China; IC design/foundries/SPE lower in
global ex China. China's AI basket is dominated by CSPs (65% of mkt cap) and then SPE (8%)
vs the US being dominated by CSPs (46%) and IC design (26%). China's IC design (dominated
by newly listed GPU design co.s), foundries (SMIC+Hua Hong) and SPE stocks trade at a
significant premium to its US peers, while Chinese CSPs, AI apps (software) and IDCs trade at
a discount. That partly drove our positive view on VNET, GDS and Kingdee.
China's AI model performance gap vs the US narrowed further to 6%, thanks to Zhipu's GLM
4.7. Zhipu's GLM 4.7 released on Dec 22 became the best-performing LLM of China, and helped
China further narrow the performance gap with the best US model (Gemini 3 Pro) to 6% (from
8% in Nov). In 4Q25, Chinese flagship LLMs have improved faster than most of their US peers. 5
Chinese LLMs published new releases with an avg increase in intelligence score of 10.8, while
2 US LLMs released new versions with an avg rise in intelligence score of 6.0.
A new supportive AI policy from the gov boosted sentiment.  On Dec 25, the MIIT together
with 7 other central gov authorities issued the Implementation Plan on the "AI+ Manufacturing"
Special Action, which targets to 1) deploy 3-5 general LLMs in manufacturing, 2) develop
1,000 cutting-edge industrial agents, 3) curate 100 high-quality industrial datasets and 500
benchmark use cases, and 4) cultivate 2-3 global ecosystem leaders and 1,000 benchmark
companies. Moreover, the gov wants to focus on 5 industries for AI deployment: 1) raw
materials, 2) equipment manufacturing, 3) consumer goods, 4) electronics and 5) software &
IT services.
The US allows the sale of H200 but no clarity from the Chinese gov yet; either way positive
for IDC.  On Dec 8, Trump announced approval for NVDA to sell H200 chips to China. But
China has not decided if it would approve such purchase. Even so, industry checks indicate
Bytedance/BABA are planning a total of 2GW of IDC orders, likely for 1H26 and using chips
in inventory.

China AI Mkt Cap Risen US$732bn Since DS, Implying 253x Incremental P/E
▪ Since the launch of DeepSeek R1 in Jan 2025, our basket 
of 33 listed China AI stocks’ market cap increased by 
~US$732bn (up 88%) to US$1.8trn as of Jan 5, 2026. 
That excludes all recent AI-related IPOs.
▪ Consensus 2026 rev/NP of this basket has risen by 
US$20bn/3bn, or 6%/4% during this period, implying 
the market attached a 253x to the incremental 2026E 
forecast earnings. 
▪ CSPs received the largest absolute market cap increase 
of US$486bn, up 75%, implying incremental P/E at 181x. 
But SPEs saw 1821x incremental PE, the highest 
subsector.
▪ Both AI apps (pure AI and ERP) and foundries recorded 
negative incremental PE driven by a fall in consensus 
profit forecasts, while their market cap has risen by 55% 
and 144%, respectively, since the emergence of 
DeepSeek.
▪ 2025 is the first year of AI-driven re-rating in China. 
Hence the incremental PE is naturally higher given its 
early stage.

LLM Apps Serve as Key User Access That Unifies Fragmented Apps and Fosters AI Agents
▪ We believe LLM Apps are important for model vendors mainly because:
▪ Securing the AI Entrance and Ecosystem: AI vendors’ core ambition is to create the AI entrance or "AI OS" (Operating 
System) of the future, which is expected to replace traditional entrances like browsers or application stores. Companies 
aim to build a "Super App" or platform ecosystem to unify previously fragmented applications. Alibaba, for instance, views 
its "Qwen" app as a "life entrance" designed to integrate its vast ecosystem, including commerce, payment, and local 
services, into a single user entrance.
▪ Platform for Agentic AI: The development of large models has progressed from merely understanding language to 
being capable of executing complex tasks (known as Agentic AI or AI Agent). AI vendors could provide AI assistants that 
not only chat with users, but also genuinely "do things" for the user, such as generating elaborate PPTs or completing 
complex workflows.
▪ Driving New Revenue and Growth: For many major players, particularly those facing slowdowns in core businesses (like 
Baidu's search market), LLM apps could provide new source of revenue generation. Although unlike US vendors that are 
already monetizing on different tier accounts, Chinese vendors could still find way to monetize via VAS, such as voice 
recording and transcribing, video creation etc.
