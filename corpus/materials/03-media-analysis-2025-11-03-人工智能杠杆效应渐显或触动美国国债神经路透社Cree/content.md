LONDON, Nov 3 (Reuters) - Seemingly Teﬂon U.S. Treasuries
have good reason to sit up and take notice of another twist in the
AI story last week, one that could add pressure alongside a more
hesitant Federal Reserve. What if the corporate AI spending
spree now starts to compete with the government for borrowed
funds?
伦敦，11 ⽉ 3 ⽇（路透社）——看似坚不可摧的美国国债有
充分理由警惕上周⼈⼯智能领域的⼜⼀变局，这⼀变局可能
与更加犹豫不决的美联储共同施压。如果企业的⼈⼯智能⽀
出狂潮现在开始与政府争夺借贷资⾦，该怎么办？
Amid all the hoopla around another blistering quarter of AI
investment plans from America's megacaps, Meta's $30 billion
bond sale jumped out as a sign that not all of the hyper-
spending is being funded by deep wells of cash. Leverage is
creeping into a capex boom that's estimated to hit some $400
billion overall this year alone.
在美国科技巨头公布⼜⼀轮令⼈瞠⽬的⼈⼯智能投资计划之
际，Meta 公司 300 亿美元的债券发⾏格外引⼈注⽬，这表
明并⾮所有巨额⽀出都来⾃深厚的现⾦储备。杠杆正悄然潜
⼊这场资本⽀出热潮——仅今年⼀年，预计总规模就将达到
约 4000 亿美元。

科技巨头的⼈⼯智能投资已膨胀⾄数千亿美元规模

parent Meta (META.O)
 has borrowed before on bond markets
in recent years but Thursday's six-part jumbo debt sale was its
biggest ever and is aimed at funding the company's huge data
center in Louisiana. It was the biggest investment-grade bond
sale in two years.
作为"七巨头"科技与⼈⼯智能先锋阵营成员，Facebook ⺟公
司 Meta 近年来曾多次发债，但周四分六部分发⾏的巨额债
券规模创下其历史之最，旨在为路易斯安那州巨型数据中⼼
提供资⾦。这是两年来规模最⼤的投资级债券发⾏。
Its joint venture in the mega Louisiana plant known as
"Hyperion" is with Blue Owl Capital, which in the previous week
raised $27 billion in the biggest single-tranche bond deal on
record.
其在路易斯安那州名为"Hyperion"的超级⼯⼚合资项⽬由
Blue Owl Capital 参与合作——这家机构上周刚通过史上最⼤
规模的单⼀债券发⾏募集了 270 亿美元资⾦。
As some estimate Meta's free cash ﬂow could now be slightly
negative next year, the stock price dropped a whopping 11% on
Thursday - its biggest one-day drop in three years and lopping
more than $150 billion off its market value. It didn't bounce back
on Friday.
有分析师预估 Meta 明年的⾃由现⾦流可能转为⼩幅负数，
其股价周四暴跌 11%——创下三年来最⼤单⽇跌幅，市值蒸
发逾 1500 亿美元。周五该股也未能收复失地。

infrastructure buildouts weigh
随着 AI 基础设施建设的重压，科技巨头的⽀出增速已超过营收增⻓
Meta's modest debt-to-earnings ratio at midyear of just 0.5,
according to Moody's, and its high credit rating meant it had no
problem raising the funds.
年中穆迪数据显示，Meta 的债务与收益⽐率仅为 0.5，加之
其⾼信⽤评级，使得该公司筹资毫⽆压⼒

Treasury market as underwriters hedged their positions.
但这笔债券的规模确实在更⼴泛的国债市场引发涟漪，因为
承销商纷纷进⾏对冲操作

虽然有⼈认为 Meta 的情况在⼈⼯智能巨头中可能属于特

JOCKEYING FOR FUNDS   资⾦争夺战
On one level, creeping leverage in the AI boom raises the stakes
in what some fear may be a bubble in the making. Plowing cash
into a bet on the future is one thing - borrowing to do it means
the revolution really has to deliver and it ups the burden of proof
that the returns will follow.
从某个层⾯看，AI 热潮中逐渐攀升的杠杆率加剧了⼈们对潜
在泡沫的担忧。⽤现⾦押注未来是⼀回事——通过借贷来投
资则意味着这场⾰命必须真正兑现承诺，并提⾼了未来必有
回报的证明要求。

issuance from AI big tech ﬁrms from 2015-2025, with the 2025
column showing a seperate bar for Sept-Oct issuance and
another for loan issuance
⼀幅堆叠柱状图展示 2015-2025 年间 AI ⼤型科技企业的投
资级债券发⾏情况，其中 2025 年柱状图分为两段：9-10 ⽉
发⾏量和贷款发⾏量
But it also harks to a wider issue of competition for capital - not
least with the U.S. Treasury as it pursues its own vast fundraising
needs.
但这同时也引出了⼀个更⼴泛的资本竞争问题——尤其是与
美国财政部⾃身庞⼤的融资需求之间形成的竞争关系。
And here's where the picture has changed in recent years.
这正是近些年形势发⽣变化的关键所在。
"Re-industrializing" America now appears to be transforming
previously cash-rich tech and services ﬁrms that have dominated
the landscape for more than a decade into bricks-and-mortar
and energy investors like their predecessors. The country's
aggregate corporate balance sheet may shift back dramatically
too as a result.
美国当前的"再⼯业化"趋势，正将过去⼗余年占据主导地位
的现⾦充裕型科技服务企业，转变为类似其前身的实体产业
与能源投资者。这⼀转变可能导致美国企业整体资产负债表
发⽣剧烈重构。
Jason Thomas at Carlyle spotlighted this earlier this year,
pointing out a shift in the aggregate U.S. corporate proﬁle
between 2009 and through the pandemic.
凯雷投资集团的杰森·托⻢斯今年早些时候就曾指出，2009
年⾄疫情期间美国企业整体特征发⽣了显著变化。
Rather than borrowing 15% of GDP from investors as they had
done in prior decades, he calculated, U.S. ﬁrms found
themselves lending 22% of GDP in the form of cash hoards,
share buybacks and special dividends.
据他测算，与过去数⼗年间向投资者举债相当于 GDP15%的
情况不同，美国企业通过现⾦储备、股票回购和特别股息等
⽅式，反⽽向市场提供了相当于 GDP22%的资⾦。
In the process, that freed up signiﬁcant amounts of private
capital to fund the government's expanding debt piles - further
cosseted by a long period of near-zero Fed interest rates and
which helped cap debt servicing costs for Treasury.
这⼀过程中释放的⼤量私⼈资本，为政府不断膨胀的债务提
供了融资⽀持——美联储⻓期维持近零利率的政策环境进⼀
步助推了这种趋势，从⽽帮助财政部有效控制了债务偿付成
本。
If the AI mega spend now burns away that cash and even sees a
renewed corporate borrowing expansion, then the still-rising
Treasury debt load may soon ﬁnd itself in a beauty contest with
the world's biggest companies for investor capital. And it may
have to pony up a lot more in interest payments in the process.
如果当前⼈⼯智能领域的巨额⽀出消耗掉这些现⾦储备，甚
⾄引发新⼀轮企业借贷扩张，那么持续攀升的美国国债可能
很快将与全球最⼤型企业展开⼀场吸引投资者资⾦的"选美
⽐赛"。在此过程中，财政部恐怕不得不⼤幅提⾼利息⽀
付。
Last week's developments won't shift the dial on their own. And
the Fed calculus and growth trajectory remain central factors.
上周的事态发展本身不会改变局势。美联储的政策权衡与经
济增⻓轨迹仍是核⼼因素。
But the direction of travel on corporate leverage could well alter
the playing ﬁeld in the years to come.
但企业杠杆率的演变⽅向，很可能在未来数年改变整个竞争
格局。
