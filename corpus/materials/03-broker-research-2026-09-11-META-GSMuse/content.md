The Introduction of Muse Al: Implications &Framing a Potential “Conversation-to-Action” Paradigm Shift
META
12m Price Target: \$725.00

With the introduction of several action-based consumer agents in the last few weeks (Spear Street Technology's Instinct; Meta's Muse Al; etc.), the consumer Al landscape has begun the process of undergoing a fundamental paradigm shift, transitioning rapidly from passive, conversational chatbots to proactive, autonomous agents capable of executing real-world tasks. We see the industry moving from simply answering questions to completing complex, high-value personal workflows such as travel booking, bill negotiation, calendar management, shopping,and administrative tasks.
Muse is Meta's fullscale personal Al agent app/website.We'd highlight a few key details/takeaways from the launch:
Interface and Access: Users interact with Muse through dedicated iOS and Android applications, the web (muse.ai),and WhatsApp. Meta designed the product around existing messaging behaviors to minimize the learning curve.
Capabilities: Powered by the Muse Spark 1.3 model family, which is optimized for long-horizon agentic tasks,Muse can autonomously browse the web, manage workflows, fill out forms,make purchases,and coordinate long-term goals.
Monetization: Meta has initially launched the Muse product as a paid subscription (Meta's first major foray into paid consumer Al subscriptions). Current subscription tiers include: Free Tier (capped at 100 million weekly tokens); Power Tier (\$2O/month for 500 million weekly tokens and faster response times); and Maximum Tier (\$1oO/month for 3 billion weekly tokens and priority access).We see the potential for additional monetization avenues over time, including commerce (agentic commerce capabilities with potential to charge a take rate over time) and/or advertising.
