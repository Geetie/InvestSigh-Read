DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Internal Revenue Service

Internal Revenue Service Advisory Council; Public Meeting

AGENCY: Internal Revenue Service, Department of Treasury.

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Internal Revenue Service Advisory Council will hold a 
public meeting.

DATES: Wednesday, November 18, 2026.

ADDRESSES: 1111 Constitution Ave. NW, Washington, DC 20224.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Anna Millikan, Office of National 
Public Liaison, at 202-317-6564 or send an email to 
<a href="/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#732306111f1a103f1a121a001c1d331a01005d141c05"><span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="c090b5a2aca9a38ca9a1a9b3afae80a9b2b3eea7afb6">[email&#160;protected]</span></a>.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Pursuant to the Federal Advisory Committee 
Act, the Internal Revenue Service announces the Internal Revenue 
Service Advisory Council (IRSAC) will hold a public meeting on 
Wednesday, November 18, 2026, from 11:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Eastern 
Time.
    The meeting will be held in person at 1111 Constitution Ave. NW, 
Washington, DC. Members of the public planning to attend should 
register by November 13 by contacting Anna Millikan at 202-317-6564 or 
sending an email to <a href="/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#6c3c190e00050f20050d051f03022c051e1f420b031a"><span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="ffaf8a9d93969cb3969e968c9091bf968d8cd1989089">[email&#160;protected]</span></a>.
    Issues to be discussed may include, but are not limited to: IRS 
Resources Enabling Increased Revenue Collection; Reducing Compliance 
Friction; Ghost Preparers; Tax-Related Identity Theft Resolution: 
Transparency, Timing, and Resource Dedication; Elective Withholding by 
Partnerships for Partners; Simplification of Form W-4 and Changes to 
Protect Employee Privacy; Processing of Penalty and Interest Abatement 
Requests; Use of Artificial Intelligence by Tax Return Preparers: 
Professional Responsibility, Confidentiality, and the Use of Taxpayer 
Information; Worker Classification and Form SS-8, Determination of 
Worker Status for Purposes of Federal Employment Taxes and Income Tax 
Withholding; Small Business Tax Literacy: Focus on Individuals with 
Business Income; Electronic Filing and Processing of Form 8802: 
Application for US Residency Certification and Digitalization of Form 
6166: Certification of US Tax Residency; Form 1099-DIV: Dividends and 
Distributions Improvements for US Federal Government Obligations for 
Regulated Investment Companies; Tax Treatment of Prediction Markets 
Business Activities and Associated Gains & Losses; Rationalization and 
Modernization of the Form W-2G Regime; Large Corporations Access to 
Electronic Transcripts; TIN Match Expansion; Elective Form 1099 
Reporting in Lieu of Schedule K-1 Reporting; Corporate Taxpayer 
Identity Protection Framework; Modernizing Administration of Certain 
Passive Foreign Investment Companies (PFICs); Collection: Improve Early 
Identification of Non-Filed Employment Tax Returns and Collection of 
Unpaid Employment Taxes; Artificial Intelligence Use for Tax Exempt and 
Governmental Entities;

[[Page 67910]]

Broaden the IRS Interpretation of ``Essential Government Function'' 
Under Section 7871 of the Internal Revenue Code; Promoting the Use of 
IRS Digital Communications; 410(b) Coverage Testing Grace Period; 
Extending Tax Status Regulations for Wholly-Owned Tribal Business 
Entities to Partially-Owned Tribal Business Entities; Suspension of 
Beard Returns; Strengthening the Analytical Basis for Addressing Earned 
Income Tax Credit Returns that Report Large Schedule C Losses; OBBBA 
Refundable Adoption Tax Credit Education and Outreach Strategy; 
Unapplied Payments--Secondary SSN; Engaging and Encouraging Saver's 
Match Participation; Preventing Tax Scams and Fraud Suffered by Low-to-
Moderate Income Taxpayers by Enhancing and Expanding the Successful 
VITA Program; Improving Business CAF Functionality to Reduce 
Authorization Burden and Expand Digital Representation; and Math Error 
Notice Drafts, Format and Clarity--Initial Reactions. The meeting 
agenda will be posted online prior to the meeting at the IRSAC web 
page, <a href="http://www.irs.gov/irsac">www.irs.gov/irsac</a>.
    Should you wish the IRSAC to consider a written statement germane 
to the Council's work, file the statement by sending an email to 
<a href="/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f9a98c9b95909ab59098908a9697b9908b8ad79e968f"><span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="4c1c392e20252f00252d253f23220c253e3f622b233a">[email&#160;protected]</span></a> by November 13, 2026.

    Dated: October 26, 2026.
Margaret A. Romaniello,
Designated Federal Officer, IRSAC, Office of National Public Liaison, 
Internal Revenue Service.
