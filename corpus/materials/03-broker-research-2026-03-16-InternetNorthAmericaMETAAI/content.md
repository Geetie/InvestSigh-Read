META is latest in series of big tech companies beginning to drive 
further efficiency from GenAI-related tools. Reports suggesting 
20% workforce cuts could represent 5-10% upside to '27 EBIT, 
provide higher EBIT floor through investment and torque as 
revenue investments yield. OW, $825 PT. 
Big Tech Re-engineering its Workforces for a GenAI Enabled World…In 10 
Debates to Shape the Sector and Key Picks in ‘26, we detailed the opportunity and 
importance for big cap tech to be leaders in '26 in driving further efficiency from 
GenAI-related tool adoption and labor force adjustments. Two and a half months 
into '26 and tech seems to be delivering...at an accelerating pace. XYZ reduced its 
workforce by ~40%, ORCL is said to be potentially (unconfirmed) considering cuts  
of  20-30k heads (or ~12-18% of workforce), TEAM reduced its workforce by ~10%, 
ADSK by ~7%, and as of last week AMZN has been said to be potentially  reducing its 
white collar workforce by ~15k (mid-single digits percent of corp. workforce) …for 
the third straight quarter (also unconfirmed). In effect, big tech is re-engineering its 
workforces for the future GenAI world. 
...With META the Latest with Another Reduction in Workforce... And now this 
weekend media reports (see here) indicate META is planning to reduce its workforce 
by 20% (or ~16k). Note that this follows the ~21k (24% of workforce at the time) 
headcount that META reduced in '22/'23 so we do not believe META has a bloated 
cost structure. Rather we believe this is driven by an ongoing focus on efficiency and 
efforts to offset accelerating investments in GenAI (capex and opex) and improving 
internal tools that are driving efficiency. Most recently, META management noted a 
“30% increase in output per engineer since the start of 2025, with the majority of 
that growth coming from adoption of agentic coding,” but we believe META is 
pushing next generation tools throughout its organization (engineers, G&A, sales 
& marketing, etc.) to drive productivity.
...Which Put Higher Floors on EPS (Near-term) and Provides More Torque as 
Revenue Investments Yield (Long-term): This reported reduction can protect 
earnings power through this investment cycle as headcount related expenses still 
make up ~80% of META's total opex...and we estimate that the elimination of ~16k 
employees could drive anywhere from $3bn-$13bn in potential savings, depending 
on the level of employees impacted. We caution investors from using the ~$1.2mn  
ex-D&A/ads opex per employee in ’25 as the savings rate given the influx of more 
expensive GenAI engineers in ’25.  For context, estimates suggest META engineers  
“only” make anywhere from ~$200k-$800k annually in the entry-level to mid-level 
ranges.
