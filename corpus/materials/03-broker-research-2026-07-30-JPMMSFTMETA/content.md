Investor Feedback: Most investors tonight were unsurprised that the stock was down, but were largely surprised
at the magnitude of the current decline (-8%). Investors broadly acknowledged that Zuckerberg wasn’t specific
enough in his outlook for a range of new AI products – and that was the most oft-cited complaint. Commonly used
adjectives included ‘underwhelming’ and ‘generic’. His prepared remarks amounted to ‘trust me’ – and in this
market that isn’t nearly enough. As for 2027 EPS estimates, for most investors these barely moved (for now).
Maybe they went down a low single digit percentage. Most remain in the $33 to $36 range – but if you’re not
beating and/or changing the narrative in this market environment, your stock is getting punished near-term.
投资者反馈：今晚多数投资者对股价下跌并不意外，但普遍对当前跌幅（-8%）之大感到震惊。投资者
普遍认为扎克伯格对一系列新 AI 产品的展望不够具体——这是最常被提及的抱怨。常用形容词包
括"令人失望"和"泛泛而谈"。他的开场陈述本质上就是"相信我"——但在当前市场环境下，这远远不
够。至于 2027 年每股收益预期，对多数投资者而言（目前）几乎未作调整。或许仅下调了低个位数百
分比。多数预期仍维持在 33 至 36 美元区间——但若在当前市场环境中未能实现业绩超预期和/或改变
市场叙事，股价短期内必将遭受惩罚。
The one comment from Susan that seemed to spook some people was her comment about growing compute
capacity in 2028, which shouldn’t come as a surprise to anyone, but was viewed as a negative given that we still
don’t know how exactly big the 2027 Capex number will ultimately be. Finally, investors wanted Zuckerberg to
provide a roadmap to take your 2027/2028 Revenues higher – and he didn’t.  Yes, he teased that we will
see more specifics on the AI business ‘soon’, but in this market that isn’t soon enough…
苏珊关于 2028 年计算能力增长的评论似乎让一些人感到不安，这其实并不令人意外，但被视为负面因

Schilsky’s Take: Do I trust Zuckerberg? Yes – mostly. Do investors in this market environment? Nope. Definitely
not. As such, he’s going to have to deliver on some combination of the Neocloud, LLM API and
business/consumer agents businesses before investors give him the benefit of the doubt. Now, are investors
‘punishing’ META by layering in all of the Opex and Capex related to Zuckerberg’s AI largesse without modeling in
related Revenue? You bet. But it won’t matter until he executes. It’s coming. Be patient. But I also think it’s clear
that investors are starting to have déjà vu as they implicitly compare MSL to FRL. Do I think that’s fair?
No. Not at all. AI in all forms demonstrably represents a massive TAM. You could neverhave said that about the
metaverse. But Zuckerberg is playing catch-up and he has little tangible to show for it – yet. But once it does
show up, I think it’s clear the stock could significantly re-rate from here…I see where MSFT is trading pre-market
as investors reconsider Softy’s place in an AI-focused world. I suspect we will have a similar moment for META in
the coming months – it’s just going to take more time.
Schilsky 的观点：我相信扎克伯格吗？是的——大部分相信。在这个市场环境下的投资者呢？不，绝
对不信。因此，他必须在 Neocloud、LLM API 以及商业/消费者代理业务方面取得一定成果，才能让
投资者给予他信任。那么，投资者是否因为将扎克伯格 AI 慷慨投入相关的所有运营支出和资本支出纳
入考量，却没有计入相应收入，而在"惩罚"META？确实如此。但在他拿出实际成果之前，这都无关紧
要。成果即将到来，请耐心等待。不过我也认为，投资者在潜意识中将 MSL 与 FRL 相提并论时，开
始产生似曾相识的感觉。我认为这公平吗？不，一点也不。各种形式的 AI 显然代表着巨大的可寻址市
场。你永远无法对元宇宙做出同样的评价。但扎克伯格正在追赶，而且目前还拿不出多少实质性的成
果——暂时如此。然而一旦成果显现，我认为这只股票很可能会从这里开始显著重估……我看到微软
在盘前交易中的表现，因为投资者正在重新思考 Softy 在 AI 主导世界中的地位。我怀疑未来几个月
META 也会经历类似的时刻——只是需要更多时间。
