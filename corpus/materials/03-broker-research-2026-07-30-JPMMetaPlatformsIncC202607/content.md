Meta Platforms Inc
Continued Ad Strength, But Limited Visibility Into Newer Forms of AI Monetization; Raising 2027 Capex Meaningfully, With More Optionality in 2028; Remain Neutral; \$640 PT
Meta shares traded down \~7.5% post-close as visibility into monetizing AI spend beyond advertising remains limited. 2Q revenue growth (+27% FXN) & the 3Q guide (+20%-26% FXN)—while still strong—came in slightly below expectations, but the focus coming out of earnings likely remains on Meta’s ability to prove out returns on heavy AI capex beyond advertising. We believe sentiment around Meta’s AI strategy had improved in recent weeks driven by signs of external AI monetization & model progress with Muse Spark 1.1. Accordingly, investors were looking for greater clarity on the timing and magnitude of newer opportunities. However, we did not come away learning much incremental on the developer API, consumer/business agents, or potential plans to directly monetize compute. Additionally, Meta did not provide any commentary on 2027 capex beyond that the company is focused on maximizing 2026 and 2027 capacity, with more flexibility in 2028 and beyond. As such, we expect Meta to continue to invest heavily N-T & we now project 2027 capex of \$243B (+70% Y/Y), while our top-line estimates remain largely unchanged, thereby putting greater pressure on FCF. Overall, we remain encouraged by Meta’s continued headroom in core ranking & recommendation improvements, model progression, & potential new forms of AI monetization, but we still need greater clarity on the product pipeline & spending outlook. We remain Neutral rated and are establishing a December 2027 PT of \$640 based on \~18x our 2028E GAAP EPS of \$35.44.
• AI-Driven Engagement & Ad Monetization Gains Continue. 2Q Ad revenue grew +27% Y/Y (+26% FXN) driven by AI-related engagement and monetization improvements, with impressions +14% Y/Y and pricing +12% Y/Y. 2Q global time spent on IG grew a DD% Y/Y, with FB video time spent increasing 9% Y/Y globally and 10% in U.S./Canada. Improvements to recommendation systems continue to drive greater content relevancy & incremental engagement, while enhanced ranking & sequence learning drove an \~8.3% uplift in clicks & \~16% greater conversions on FB. Meta continues to simplify campaign creation & management for advertisers, with 9M+ small businesses using at least one AI creative tool in 2Q (vs. 8M+ in 1Q), and Advantage+ is now operating at a \$75B+ ARR. While advances in ads ranking & recommendation models have supported Revenue growth, Meta believes there is headroom to continue improving recommendation initiatives and monetization efficiency, especially as the company integrates LLMs across the ecosystem over time.
• No Change to AI Investment Areas, but External Monetization Still Early. Meta outlined key focus areas to driving ROIC across AI initiatives including 1) accelerating the core business; 2) developing personal agents which will be the foundation of a new wave of products and revenue lines; and 3) enterprise, which includes business agents, APIs, and selling compute directly. However, Meta shared only limited information on the Meta Model API and directly monetizing compute, which are Meta’s first forms of external AI monetization. Summary Investment Thesis and Valuation Investment Thesis
We believe Meta’s virtual ownership of the social graph, strong competitive moat, and focus on the user experience position it to become an enduring blue-chip company built for the long term. Meta reach and engagement continue to drive network effects, and its targeting abilities provide significant value to advertisers. META is delivering significant AI-driven ad ranking & recommendation gains, supporting greater engagement, ROAS, & revenue growth. Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks
Meta Platforms Inc (Neutral; Price Target: \$640.00)
Investment Thesis
We believe Meta’s virtual ownership of the social graph, strong competitive moat, and focus on the user experience position it to become an enduring blue-chip company built for the long term. Meta reach and engagement continue to drive network effects, and its targeting abilities provide significant value to advertisers. META is delivering significant AI-driven ad ranking & recommendation gains, supporting greater engagement, ROAS, & revenue growth. However, AI monetization remains less proven vs. Internet peers and heavy capex drives multi-year FCF declines. We look for updates across AI execution, model launches & efficacy, & agentic product rollout. Our December 2027 PT of \$640 is based on \~18x our 2028E GAAP EPS of \$35.44.
Valuation
We are establishing a December 2027 PT of \$640 (vs. prior \$725 December 2026 PT) based on \~18x our 2028E GAAP EPS of \$35.44, further supported by our DCF model. We apply a slight premium to the SPX as we have greater confidence in Meta’s strong top-line growth and ongoing cost efficiencies. We use this valuation approach as we believe it appropriately balances Meta’s valuation relative to its growth and industry peers.
Risks to Rating and Price Target
Upside risks include: 1) AI Ad initiatives drive engagement, revenue growth, & monetization; 2) Muse Spark & MSL model launches resonate and drive model efficacy, user growth, & engagement; 3) ROAS gains drive greater ad spend and market share gains, supporting revenue growth; & 4) Investments ease & cost discipline drives greater than expected GAAP EPS & FCF.
Downside risks include: 1) AI investments are heavy & monetization lags, which weighs on GAAP OI, GAAP EPS, & FCF; 2) slower than expected revenue growth limits AI monetization upside; 3) AI product & model launches do not resonate, weighing on engagement, revenue, & margins; 4) competition for online and mobile ad dollars from Google, TikTok, OpenAI, and other online advertising companies; & 5) regulatory litigations & charges.
