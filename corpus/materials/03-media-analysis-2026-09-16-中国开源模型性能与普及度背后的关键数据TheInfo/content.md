Open-source AI developments have kept The Information’s reporting corps busy this summer, with news on regulation, Meta Platforms’ return to open source, and the rise of Chinese labs, all culminating with Nvidia’s \$12.9 billion purchase of the Hugging Face and the potential \$40 billion valuation for startup Thinking Machines Lab.
今年夏天，开源人工智能领域的发展让《信息报》的报道团队忙得不可开交，相关新闻包括监管</b>动态、元宇宙平台公司（MetaPlatforms）回归开源的举措、中国实验室的崛起，最终以英伟达（Nvidia）以129亿美元收购哈辛格费斯（Hugging Face），以及初创公司思维机器实验室（Thinking Machines Lab）获得400亿美元的潜在估值达到高潮。
Open source’s rise raises urgent questions about the geopolitics and the relative costs and performance of open and closed models. To better understand the landscape, I’ve been examining open source models as a supply chain, digging into a rich data trove from Hugging Face that I think is under-discussed.
开源的兴起引发了关于地缘政治以及开源与闭源模型相对成本和性能的紧迫问题。为了更好地了解这一格局，我一直将开源模型视为一条供应链进行研究，并深入挖掘了来自 Hugging Face 的海量数据宝库——我认为这一数据宝库的相关讨论还不够充分。

A core advantage of open models is the ability to download and tailor them to a specific application or task rather than utilizing a generally trained closed model. The Hugging Face data captures more than 870,000 of these open-source “instances” over four-and-a-half years that show which underlying open model a developer built upon.
开源模型的一个核心优势是能够下载并针对特定应用或任务对其进行定制，而非使用经过通用训练的闭源模型。在四年半的时间里，Hugging Face 平台的数据记录了超过 87 万个这类开源“实例”，这些实例展示了开发者基于哪款基础开源模型进行开发。

China dominates not only intelligence gains but also which models developers choose to build on
中国不仅在智能领域的发展成果上占据主导地位，还主导着开发者选择以哪些模型为基础进行开发
Developers increasingly stick with existing opensource models, creating inertia against new challengers.
开发者越来越倾向于使用现有的开源模型，这对新的挑战者形成了阻碍。
Chinese models narrowed open source’s performance gap behind the closedsource frontier by two thirds in two years. We know that models from China have become much better. This data set shows how that convergence accelerated as models became more complex and training methods evolved.
在两年时间里，中国模型将闭源前沿技术与开源之间的性能差距缩小了三分之二。我们知道中国的模型已经进步了很多。该数据集展示了随着模型变得更加复杂、训练方法不断优化，这种性能趋同的速度是如何加快的。
From the fourth quarter of 2022—when ChatGPT launched—through the third quarter of 2024, when the industry was focused mainly on scaling up models’ size, the best new open models were as good as the best frontier closed models nine months prior, based on benchmark evaluations like Artificial Analysis’ Intelligence Index. Over the next year, when reasoning models were the focus, that narrowed substantially. By the current agent-focused period, which has emerged since the end of last year, the gap is only about three months.
从2022年第四季度ChatGPT推出到2024年第三季度，这一阶段行业主要聚焦于扩大模型规模，根据人工智能分析公司的智能指数等基准评估结果，当时最优秀的新型开源模型，性能与九个月前最顶尖的前沿闭源模型相当。在接下来的一年里，推理模型成为研发重点，这一差距大幅缩小。而自去年年底以来进入智能体聚焦阶段，目前这一差距仅约为三个月。

Open-source AI models by Alibaba, Meta and Google account for about half of the derivative-model building by professional developers. And Alibaba, the most prolific maker of open models in recent years, is by far the biggest of those three.
阿里巴巴、Meta和谷歌的开源人工智能模型占专业开发者构建衍生模型数量的约一半。而阿里巴巴作为近年来开源模型产出最多的厂商，是这三家企业中规模最大的。
Existing open-source models are becoming more entrenched among developers. The data shows that developers who build on open source increasingly stick with the same model, fine-tuned to their needs, rather than cycling through a deluge of new releases. That has meaningful implications for the efforts of U.S. open-source model makers—from Nvidia to Thinking Machine Lab—to catch up to Chinese rivals in not just performance but usage.
现有的开源模型在开发者群体中的地位愈发稳固。 数据显示，基于开源技术进行开发的人员，越来越倾向于坚守同一套经过自身需求微调的模型，而非频繁更换大量新推出的模型。这对美国开源模型厂商（从英伟达到思维机器实验室）而言，有着重要的影响—他们不仅要在性能上追赶中国竞争对手，还需在使用量上实现赶超。

Take Alibaba’s Qwen model family. Its current-generation model, released in January, accounts for 15% of all developer activity on Hugging Face with Qwen, while the two-year-old predecessor still accounts for 20%.
以阿里巴巴的通义千问模型家族为例。其今年1月发布的当前版本模型在Hugging Face平台上占所有基于通义千问的开发者活动的15%，而两年前的前代模型仍占20%。

All of that means that, while U.S. open source companies including Thinking Machines Lab and Reflection AI—and their common backer, Nvidia, with its Nemotron initiative—are racing to develop more powerful open models to compete with those from China, they will be pushing against considerable inertia to gain wider popularity in usage.
所有这一切意味着，尽管思维机器实验室（Thinking Machines Lab）和反射人工智能等美国开源企业——以及它们的共同投资方、凭借其内莫特龙（Nemotron）计划的英伟达——正竞相开发更强大的开源模型以与中国企业的产品一较高下，但它们要想在应用中获得更广泛的普及，仍需克服相当大的惯性阻力。
