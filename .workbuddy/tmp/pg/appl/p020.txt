ii. Early-period D1, D3, and D7 user data was roughly 20% below the D7- targeted strategy.
iii. By D21, the curve converges with the D7 strategy.
b. D7 and D28 run in parallel without conflict and cover different audiences.
i. D28 helps gaming companies identify users with strong purchasing power across both early and late lifecycle stages.
ii. Particularly well-suited to SLG titles.
5. Summary
Aggregating the above channel checks, we remain broadly constructive on Vector growth and believe a 15% QoQ growth pace is sustainable. However, we have not yet seen Unity actively guide advertisers to reallocate budget from IronSource to Unity (Vector).
