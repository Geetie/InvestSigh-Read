i. The company went through a lengthy negotiation with AppLovin during Black Friday, alongside a legal-led data security review.
1. The company was concerned about potential data leakage. If, for example, SKU preference data for specific users were leaked to competing e-commerce platforms, the company could face direct user churn risk.
2. The company viewed AppLovin’s e-commerce data security handling as less mature than META, GOOG, and TikTok, requiring a longer legal review process.
3. By early December, the expert believed negotiations were near completion, and the company maintained the position that any data leakage incident would result in immediate termination of the ecommerce data collaboration.
ii. After clearing legal review, the company formally began data collaboration with AppLovin.
1. Pre-Black Friday December: began formally returning shopping behavior data to AppLovin, but limited to a small number of categories and brands.
2. Throughout 2026: gradually expanded to additional brands and categories and broadened the user age cohorts.
iii. Returned data includes:
1. User label data and user behavior counts (clicks, add-to-cart, browsing).
2. Purchase data and repurchase data.
3. The company does not return purchase behavior for users who entered the platform through META or TikTok; only users directly attributable to AppLovin are returned.
