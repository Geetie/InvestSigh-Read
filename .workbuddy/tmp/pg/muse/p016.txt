Healthcare: The Monetization Opportunity
Prescription purchases: A user asked Muse to find a cheaper way to fill a prescription. After finding an option, Muse messaged One Medical to have the prescription transferred and helped place the order. The user also asked it to arrange reorders when the supply was running low, combining price comparison, prescription transfer and refills in one task.
Shopping Ads: Room for Further Growth
Shopping for a work bag: A user wanted a zippered bag that cost no more than \$400, weighed under two pounds and could hold a 15-inch laptop. Muse browsed brand and retail sites and returned three options in about 15 minutes, with the choice of visiting the product pages or buying directly through Muse. The user selected a Briggs & Riley bag and asked Muse to start the Nordstrom checkout process. At the login step, however, the user became concerned about account access and completed the purchase independently. Even so, the user felt Muse had found suitable options and saved considerable time on screening.
Buying an appliance: A user needed a replacement garage freezer and gave Muse the specifications required to replace the existing unit. According to the user, Muse found the best-priced model that met the requirements, placed the order, tracked delivery and completed the product registration. Initially, the user watched every step and enabled several additional permissions along the way. After the purchase, the user said they were beginning to trust Muse with these tasks.

Public screenshots showed Muse locating the medium-size coat at \$450, then checking major retailers for a lower price.
