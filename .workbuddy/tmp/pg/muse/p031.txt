2 . CPM Growth by Ad Format
a. For Facebook and Instagram News Feed combined, CPM growth was 6% YoY in 1Q26 and 11% in 2Q26, followed by 13% in June, 16% in July and 17% in August.
b. Stories CPM growth was 10% YoY in 1Q26 and 16% in 2Q26, followed by 16% in June, 17% in July and 18% in August.
c. Reels CPM growth was 7% YoY in 1Q26 and 16% in 2Q26, followed by 17% in June, 18% in July and 17% in August.
d. CPM growth across all three formats should exceed expectations in 3Q26. Likely drivers include better use of customer data, AI-driven audience selection, bid optimization and stronger creative. Meta Business Agents are part of that, but the bigger change is broader improvement in algorithms, AI and data-driven personalized marketing, which appears to have accelerated meaningfully over the past two to three months.
