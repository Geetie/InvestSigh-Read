3. Meta Business Agents: Adoption and Revenue
a. By my estimate, WhatsApp Business currently accounts for a low-single-digit share of Meta’s total revenue. In our business, it is roughly 7%, or about three times the typical level. Our experience may point to where the broader market is heading over the next few quarters.
b. WhatsApp Business spending mainly comes from midsize and large companies using their own call centers or AI, along with some small businesses that handle messages themselves. The typical charge is 2–3 cents per message. Meta Business Agents should become the largest growth driver, but their role extends beyond getting small businesses to spend on WhatsApp.
c. When small businesses adopt Meta Business Agents, the ROI on their core Facebook and Instagram campaigns typically improves, and they increase spending. They are then encouraged to turn on AI-powered messaging. Initially, some existing budget shifts into messaging. When messaging delivers a good ROI, the business increases spending again. Adoption happens in several stages.
