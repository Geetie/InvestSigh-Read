Travel: The Monetization Opportunity
Hotel bookings: A user planning a two-night stay in New York wanted to compare IHG, Hyatt and Marriott properties, considering standard cash rates, corporate rates and points redemptions. After giving Muse the requirements, the user asked it to keep checking daily and send an alert if rates fell or another suitable nearby hotel became available. Muse also estimated the value of points redemptions to help the user decide whether to pay cash or use points.
Trip planning: Another user planning a European trip had not settled on arrival and departure cities. Muse compared flights from San Francisco to 11 Spanish cities and return flights from 10 Italian cities, searching multiple dates for both cash fares and award availability. According to the user, it ran 146 searches and presented several payment options: approximately \$890 in cash, 40,000 points plus \$455, or 15,000 points plus \$577. The user could then weigh the cash budget against available points and compare arrival cities, return cities and flight combinations without repeating searches for every city and date.
