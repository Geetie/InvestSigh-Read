Credit cards: In a public review, a user asked Muse to help choose a credit card, providing major spending categories and a ceiling on the annual fee. Muse produced a comparison with product links, allowing the user to review the terms of the shortlisted cards.
Automotive: The Monetization Opportunity
Buying a car: One user delegated several separate steps in the purchase process to Muse: researching vehicles, negotiating with dealers, obtaining insurance quotes and scheduling a pre-purchase inspection. The experience covered model selection, pricing and contact with insurers and inspection providers.
Vehicle maintenance: A user had been putting off an oil change because a loaner car was needed and availability had to be confirmed by phone. In an app-store review, the user said that after receiving the vehicle details and phone number, Muse called, booked service for the following Monday and secured a loaner. It also supplied a call transcript so the user could review what had been discussed.
Local Services: The Monetization Opportunity
Carpentry: A user described a home carpentry project to Muse and then supplied more detail. Muse found and emailed businesses and created a spreadsheet of phone numbers and appointments. According to the user, a carpenter called back about ten minutes after Muse reported contacting the businesses. The carpenter, who ran a small shop, visited the next morning to assess the job and provide a quote. The user accepted, and work began. By the time the user posted the account, the carpenter was already working at the house.
