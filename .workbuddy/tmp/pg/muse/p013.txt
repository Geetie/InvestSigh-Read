Financial Services: The Monetization Opportunity
Auto insurance: A user had repeatedly considered switching to a cheaper policy but abandoned the process because it required new forms, quotes and sales calls. This time, the user uploaded the existing policy and asked Muse to find a lower price for the same coverage. According to the user, Muse found an option in about five minutes that would save roughly \$3,500 a year, then helped purchase the new policy and cancel the old one. A task that had repeatedly been put off moved from comparison shopping to a completed switch.
