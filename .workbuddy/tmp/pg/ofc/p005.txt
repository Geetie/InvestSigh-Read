Optical Scale-up Consortium - The newly announced Optical Scale-up Consortium (OSC) extends beyond a standard-setting announcement. The longer-term implications for capital allocation and infrastructure planning are significant. The consortium’s roadmap is already circulating in the industry and will likely become more visible around GTC.
If NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Microsoft, Meta, and OpenAI are jointly defining an optical scale-up standard, that alone is a strong signal about where AI infrastructure is heading.
Supply chain checks suggest the architecture will likely center on ELS, fiber, and SiPho as the physical layer. CPO, NPO, and on-board optics could emerge as form factors. This direction acknowledges a constraint the industry has approached for some time: copper is running out of headroom for next-generation scale-up fabrics.
Once scale-up moves to optics, the traditional interconnect debates—NVLink vs UALink vs other proprietary fabrics—may become less central. The physical layer is converging on optics, with differentiation shifting higher in the stack.
One interesting detail is that Broadcom is a founding member of the optical scale-up consortium, something that would have been surprising not long ago, given its deep positioning in electrical interconnect ecosystems.
