Great update! - A few thoughts:
1) I agree that the -20% headcount reduction is a massive signal to the Street that management has its eye on expenses. Beyond just the immediate savings, it signals a cultural shift: Meta is willing to operate like a "normal" non-tech company by trimming headcount even when revenue growth is strong and margins are well above average. It’s easy to forget that when you exclude the discretionary drag of Reality Labs, Meta’s core operating margins are still distinctively robust at 49%. That is an incredibly high floor for any business.
2) I’m also very bullish on Manus and its potential to transform the WhatsApp and Messenger ecosystems. However, we have to remember that the general public—and even many institutional investors—aren't really aware of Manus yet. Investors have a habit of judging Meta based on the tools they personally use in their daily lives, which makes them slow to realize the massive value these tools provide to the actual paying customers (the advertisers).
I’m actually working on a piece right now about how Meta has quietly begun aggressively paying political content creators in a targeted bid to lure them away from TikTok. While the $135B AI Capex guide gets all the headlines and scrutiny, the CORE business is as strong as ever and is successfully finding new ways to deepen its moat.
Keep up the great work!!! -Accrued Interest
