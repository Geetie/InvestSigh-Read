For the record, in our 2023 datacenter CPU spending and market share outlook, we penned in Meta as a 7% Capex decline. Instead, they will spend \~12% more year on year. Most of this will be levered towards AI training and inference, so it will not be a significant change for CPU spending. It should be noted that Meta’s spending guidance is most levered to Arista Networks and Nvidia. A lot of incremental dollars will actually flow to Intel as well given Meta has designed Sapphire Rapids into the majority of next-generation Yosemite and all Grand Teton servers. This is due to Sapphire Rapids host of specialized accelerators such as AMX (AI inference), DSA (data streaming accelerator), IAA (in-memory analytics), DLB (dynamic loadbalancer), and QAT (quickassist technology). However, these wins at Meta won’t truly move the needle for Intel.
需要说明的是，在我们此前对2023年数据中心CPU支出与市场份额的展望中，曾将 Meta 的资本开支预测为同比下降7%。而实际情况是，Meta 的资本开支将同比增长约12%。这部分增量大多流向 AI 训练与推理，因此对 CPU 支出本身不会带来太大变化。值得注意的是，Meta 的支出指引最受益的是 Arista Networks 和英伟达（Nvidia）。由于 Meta 已经将 Sapphire Rapids 设计进了下一代 Yosemite 服务器的大部分机型以及全部Grand Teton 服务器，因此也会有相当一部分增量资金流向英特尔。这是因为 Sapphire Rapids 搭载了一整套专用加速器，包括 AMX（AI 推理加速）、DSA（Data Streaming Accelerator，数据流加速器）、IAA（In-Memory Analytics Accelerator，内存内分析加速器）、DLB（Dynamic Load Balancer，动态负载均衡器）以及 QAT（QuickAssist 加速技术）。不过，Meta 这边的这些设计中标，并不会真正为英特尔的业绩带来实质性改变。
The only other thing to mention is that the Capex that Meta is spending is doing meaningful work for shortform video uptake. Meta is firmly ahead of Google on this front, and they continue to spend to catch up to TikTok.
另外值得一提的是，Meta 投入的这些资本开支，在推动短视频渗透率方面确实起到了实质作用。在这一领域，Meta 目前明显领先于谷歌，并仍在持续投入以追赶 TikTok。
