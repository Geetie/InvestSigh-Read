2023 looks very weak for Apple tester demand. Mediatek’s and Qualcomm’s transition to 3nm is also later, slated for 2024 model year launched devices. While Teradyne has moderate sales in China, given where they sit in the supply chain, the restrictions don’t directly impact them.
2023年苹果相关的测试机需求看起来会相当疲软。联发科（MediaTek）和高通（Qualcomm）转向3nm的时间也会推迟，要到2024年款设备上市时才会落地。虽然 Teradyne 在中国有一定规模的销售，但考虑到其在产业链中所处的位置，制裁并不会对其造成直接影响。
United Microelectronics Corporation – \$UMC
联电（UMC） – \$UMC
UMC is the leading trailing edge fab in Taiwan. There is finally smoke in their numbers. They guided a 10% quarterly decrease in revenue and cut 2022 Capex by 17%. Given how far into the year this is, they are pumping the brakes hard, as we discussed in our recent deep dive into semiconductor fab buildout spending and delays.
UMC 是台湾成熟制程晶圆代工的龙头企业。他们的数据终于开始冒出警讯了：公司指引显示营收环比下滑10%，并将2022年资本开支下调17%。考虑到已经临近年末才做出这样的调整，他们这是在大力踩刹车——这一点我们在此前关于半导体晶圆厂扩产支出与延期的深度分析中也讨论过。
Foundry margins will go down structurally, especially at tier 2 foundries such as UMC.
代工厂毛利率将出现结构性下滑，尤其是像 UMC 这样的二线代工厂。
Utilization rates will dip instead of being pegged at 100% in Q4. Only 80% of their 28nm revenue is protected by long-term agreements for 2023, accounting for \~20% of their revenue. The other 20% of 28nm is not. Long-term agreements are often broken in the semiconductor world so downside is large.
第四季度的稼动率（利用率）将出现下滑，而不再维持在100%满载。在2023年，他们28nm营收中只有80%受到长期协议（LTA）保护，这部分约占其总营收的20%；28nm营收中另外20%则没有长期协议保护。而在半导体行业，长期协议被撕毁的情况并不少见，因此下行风险很大。
The read-through is very important for GlobalFoundries and further contributes to the negative report we released on August 12th, the 6-month high for \$GFS.
这对 GlobalFoundries 的读数意义十分重要，也进一步印证了我们8月12日发布的看空报告— 当时正是\$GFS 股价的6个月 高点。
