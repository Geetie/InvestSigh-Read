Generation vendors will suggest at minimum an N+1 configuration, if not an N+1+1 configuration. An N+1 configuration maintains full generation capacity even if one generator unexpectedly shuts down, whereas an N+1+1 configuration enables this flexibility while also keeping another generator on standby to enable maintenance cycles.
