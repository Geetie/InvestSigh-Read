Different projects account for capital differently. A pure merchant power plant must recover capex via energy sales and capacity payments. Some onsite systems, though, are better compared to “powered land.” In many datacenter hotspots, plots are priced less by acreage and more by the amount of pre-approved electric service and time-to-power. In extreme cases, powered land with <18-month time-to-power is so exorbitantly priced that it’s cheaper to buy unpowered land and build onsite generation instead.
不同项目对资本核算方式各异。纯商业发电厂需通过能源销售和容量支付回收资本支出。然而，部分现场发电系统更适合与"通电地块"模式相比较。在许多数据中心热点区域，地块定价并非主要依据面积，而是基于预先获批的电力供应容量和通电时效。极端情况下，通电周期短于 18 个月的"即用型"地块价格高得离谱，反而比购置未通电土地并自建现场发电设施更为昂贵。
