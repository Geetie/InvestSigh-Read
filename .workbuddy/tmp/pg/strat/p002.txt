Google Earnings, Meta Earnings – Stratechery by Ben Thompson
Like other tech giants, Alphabet is spending heavily to advance its AI models and build data centers needed to train and run them. The company revised its estimates for capital expenditures this year to $180 billion to $190 billion, up from $175 billion to $185 billion.
和其他科技巨头一样，Alphabet 正斥巨资推进其人工智能模型的研发，并建设训练和运行这些模型所需的数据中心。该公司将今年的资本支出预期从1750亿至1850亿美元上调至1800亿至1900亿美元。
Google’s Cloud division, which sells access to AI platforms and computing power, has grown significantly as the company works to expand its customer base. The Cloud unit had $20 billion in first-quarter revenue, a 63% increase from the same period in 2025. The company said its cloud backlog growth was driven by demand for its enterprise AI tools and its custom chips, known as Tensor Processing Units, or TPUs. It expects to record about half of the backlog as revenue over the next two years.
谷歌的云部门（销售AI平台和算力访问服务）在公司扩大客户群的过程中实现了显著增长。该云部门第一季度营收达200亿美元，较2025年同期增长63%。谷歌表示，其云业务待交付订单的增长得益于企业AI工具和定制芯片（即张量处理单元TPU）的需求。公司预计未来两年将把约一半的待交付订单转化为营收。
The headline for this Article is “Google Profit Jumps 81% as Cloud Business Booms”, and, well, that is technically true: Google did indeed report an 81% increase in profit. The more pertinent number, however, is 30%, which is how much Google’s operating profit increased. This is still extremely impressive, to be clear: not only is that a very large increase, but it significantly outpaced Google’s overall revenue growth of 22%.
这篇文章的标题是“谷歌利润随云业务蓬勃发展飙升81%”，嗯，从技术上讲这是事实:谷歌确实公布了81%的利润增长。不过，更关键的数字是30%，这是谷歌营业利润的增幅。需要明确的是，这一数字依然极为亮眼:不仅增幅十分可观，还大幅超过了谷歌22%的整体营收增长率。
