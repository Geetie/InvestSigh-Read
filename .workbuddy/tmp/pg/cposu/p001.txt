Meta Discusses AI Hardware and Co-packaged Optics Meta 探讨 AI 硬件与共封装光学
SemiAnalysis · 2022-09-15
★ Meta's power-breakdown data shows optics will swallow most of a training cluster's power at high per-node bandwidth, and Nvidia — already investing in Ayar Labs and partnering with GlobalFoundries and TSMC on co-packaged optics — is the only player positioned to solve compute, switching, and optics together.
