SNOW Acquiring Observe
This is a very interesting deal. We’ve been following this company since many years ago. Observe is a Snowflake Native company - its Data Layer has been Native based on Snowflake for a long time. In 2023 or sometime around then, Observe posted a tweet mentioning that they contributed 40mn Snowflake queries per day, accounting for 2% of Snowflake’s query volume at one point. Later, with the rapid growth of Snowpark, more lower-priced queries emerged, diluting Observe’s share of Snowflake queries.
Actually, there was another Snowflake Native startup growing even faster at the time - Lacework, also incubated by Sutter Hill - but it quickly ran into difficulties. I didn’t expect that after 2-3 years, we’d be discussing cases like Observe again.
Log data doesn’t necessarily have to be stored in log products - it’s essentially a combination of structured/semi-structured data, so it can exist in Snowflake and Databricks in structured/semi-structured form. If customers ultimately want an integrated data platform that can manage all their various data types, hoping to complete the data processing needed for data applications by reducing data copies and ETL, then naturally they can choose Snowflake and Databricks along with their Monitor, Log, and SIEM products on top.
