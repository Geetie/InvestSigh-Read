Other architectures only use subagents. In one case, 4 distinct o4-mini subagents achieved 1.3x speedup on average on existing, production grade SG Lang Kernels. Specifically, existing CUDA kernels are plucked from SG Lang and isolated such that they can be evaluated on a standalone basis. Then it passes through a loop between four agents. One agents conducts testing to validate the kernel on a pass or fail basis, and the other conducts detailed profiling to identify performance deltas. The third agent plans targeted edits, while the fourth implements them.
