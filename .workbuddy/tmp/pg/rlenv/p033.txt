Since then, many different architectures have popped up, some targeted at specific evals. As an example, researcher Jeremy Brennan leveraged a multi-agent architecture built on top of Grok 4 to get a record on ARC-AGI. The actual method is rather simple, involving Grok 4 generating instructions for each task. Then, each instruction is given to another agent that tests these instructions on the training examples. If a perfect solution emerges, these are locked in and applied to the held-out test grid. Otherwise, they enter an iterative refinement cycle.
