Interactive Brokers (IBKR US) is no less user friendly than the other major platforms but allows individuals to trade on 
market terms – a slight spread to the risk free rate and roughly less than half the total cost of margin from its competitors. 
(Note, HOOD also provides competitive rates).
Some napkin math suggest that SCHW is earning an extra $9 billion in pre-tax profit on this excess spread.
We’ve yet to try to move existing holdings across via Muse to see how that might work, but a better informed customer 
base with an agentic helper is more likely to attempt these shifts and is more likely to transfer funds or choose IBKR in the 
first place.
Money Markets  
Traditional wealth advisors have long extracted fees by providing personal reassurance to an area daunting to many: their 
finances. But now, the idea of calling up your financial advisor, and having them pitch a complex structured product or 
high-fee mutual fund seems antiquated. The first line of “expert opinion” is increasingly going to LLMs and agents.
