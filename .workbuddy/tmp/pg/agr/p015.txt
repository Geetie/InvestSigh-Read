The way in which agents use the internet, call APIs and query data can benefit vendors that we don’t / can’t know yet until 
we’ve got more insight into how people use agents and what they use them for.
Right now, the vast majority of insight we have about agentic users surrounds coders, developers and technologically savvy 
early adopters. This isn’t representative of the normal distribution of people (yes, sorry, we are calling you and ourselves 
weirdos). This is something to keep track of as things progress.
