Speculative Transaction Infrastructure Winners  
Another area that we can look to in order to deduce potential beneficiaries is suppliers and places where transaction 
infrastructure stays unchanged.
Ticketmaster / Live Nation (LYV US): Ticketmaster announced its Muse connector on September 8.
Muse can surface events and current ticket options, but final purchases take place on Ticketmaster’s marketplace. The 
potential benefit is better conversion, additional attendance and filling otherwise unsold inventory that does not require a 
new API toll.
AIG (AIG US): Link’s terms contain a specific Section 6.2 for eligible purchases using Meta’s Muse. The linked policy guide 
names New Hampshire Insurance Company, an AIG company, with Stripe as the named insured. Cover Genius/XCover 
handles the agency and claims-administration functions described in Link’s terms.
