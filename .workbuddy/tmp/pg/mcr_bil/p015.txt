While DSF was an immediate answer to Meta’s problems around AI clusters, it wasn’t long before the nonscheduled approach caught up. The shift was driven by the NICs themselves getting smarter: as AI clusters scaled through 2023 and 2024, NIC vendors began building RoCE congestion handling directly into their silicon. Once the NIC could absorb the complexity, the fabric no longer had to. Non-Scheduled Fabric (NSF) makes use of switches employing a simpler shallow-buffer switch chip like the Tomahawk 5 ASIC. Virtually every major switch vendor such as Arista, as well as white-box vendors like Celestica, sells switch boxes built around Tomahawk 5, meaning better cost optimization and a diversity of solutions.
尽管 DSF 是 Meta 应对 AI 集群问题时的一个即时解法，但没过多久，非调度式方案就迎头赶上。这一转变的驱动力来自 网卡自身变得更加智能：随着 AI 集群在 2023 年至 2024 年间不断扩容，网卡厂商开始将 RoCE拥塞处理能力直接内置进芯片。一旦网卡能够承担这部分复杂度，fabric 就不再需要承担它了。NSF（非调度式 fabric）所使用的交换机，采用的是像 Tomahawk 5 这样更简单的浅缓冲交换芯片。包括 Arista 在内的几乎所有主流交换机厂商，以及 Celestica 这样的白盒厂商，都在销售基于 Tomahawk 5 打造的交换机产品，这意味着更好的成本优化空间与更丰富的方案选择。
As Meta’s GPU clusters grew in size, it became apparent that there were scaling limits to DSF. But by the time Meta engineers realized that NSF was the better implementation, there was no time to develop it given its urgent need of capacity. DSF was deployed with engineers aware that it was a suboptimal and interim solution.
随着 Meta GPU 集群规模不断扩大，DSF 的扩展性瓶颈逐渐显现。但等到 Meta 工程师意识到 NSF 才是更优实现方式时，由于对算力扩容的迫切需求，已经没有时间重新开发。DSF 就是在工程师们明知其为次优过渡方案的情况下被部署上线的。
Based on our modeling, DSF is about 11% more expensive than NSF. DSF also forces Meta to rely only on a single vendor, whereas NSF brings vendor diversity. All the above explains why NSF is now the preferred choice for Meta, with Prometheus and Hyperion deploying a vast majority of NSF instances. Even so, DSF is not going away entirely: which fabric a cluster gets ultimately comes down to which NIC it uses. GB300 deployments run on Nvidia’s NICs and use NSF, while systems built around MTIA, Meta’s in-house accelerator with its NIC integrated directly on the chip, revert to DSF. The two approaches will co-exist at Meta for the foreseeable future.
根据我们的测算，DSF 的成本比 NSF 高出约 11%。DSF 还迫使 Meta 只能依赖单一供应商，而 NSF 则能带来供应商多元化。以上种种，解释了为何 NSF 如今已成为 Meta 的首选方案，Prometheus 与 Hyperion 部署的绝大多数实例都采用了 NSF。即便如此，DSF 也不会彻底退出舞台：一个集群最终使用哪种 fabric，取决于它所用的网卡。GB300 部署使用 Nvidia 的网卡，采用 NSF；而围绕 MTIA（Meta 自研加速卡，其网卡直接集成在芯片上）搭建的系统，则仍要沿用 DSF。这两种方案在可预见的未来将在 Meta 内部并存。
None of this means DSF was a blunder. It was the best available answer in 2024, and Meta was smart enough to pivot when a better one emerged.
这并不意味着 DSF 是一步臭棋。在 2024 年，它是当时能拿到的最佳答案，而当更好的方案出现时，Meta 也足够聪明地及时转向。
