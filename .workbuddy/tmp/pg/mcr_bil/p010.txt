Because we understand that the entirety of Meta’s GB200 fleet was in this Ariel SKU, this “optimization” left Meta’s LLM teams burdened with an inferior system to the standard SKU that Meta’s competitors purchased. This is particularly biting as GB200 was the flagship system for gen AI at the time. Unsurprisingly, there is no more Ariel for Meta’s GB300-type servers and Meta is buying the normal configuration.
据我们了解，Meta 整个 GB200机队都是这款 Ariel SKU，这项“优化”让 Meta 的 LLM 团队背负着一套劣于竞争对手所购标准 SKU 的系统。考虑到 GB200当时是生成式 AI 的旗舰系统，这一点尤其令人痛心。不出所料，到了 GB300这一代 Meta 的服务器上，Ariel 已经销声匿迹，Meta 转而采购标准配置。
Upcoming AMD Disasterous Decision
即将到来的 AMD 灾难性决策
However, we see this now popping up again with AMD’s MI450X. In AMD’s Q1 2026 earnings call, Lisa Su confirmed that AMD is providing Meta with a custom GPU based on the MI450. We have reported this for many months in the Accelerator Model. This custom Meta MI450 is a cut down version of the full MI450X with half the compute silicon per package and number of HBM stacks. The HBM4 is also downgraded to 8- Hi HBM vs 12-Hi in the standard SKU. Given Meta’s Helios rack has the same number of switches, we assume that the amount of I/O is the same.
然而，我们现在看到同样的模式正在 AMD 的 MI450X 上重演。在 AMD 2026年第一季度财报电话会上，苏姿丰确认 AMD 将向 Meta 提供一款基于 MI450的定制 GPU。我们已在 Accelerator Model 中持续追踪报道这一情况数月之久。这款 Meta 定制版 MI450，是完整版 MI450X 的阉割版：每颗封装的计算芯粒数量减半，HBM 堆栈数量也减半。HBM4规格也从标准 SKU 的12层堆叠降级为8层堆叠。鉴于 Meta 的 Helios 机柜交换机数量不变，我们判断 I/O 总量应当保持一致。

By halving the compute and memory silicon, the goal is to achieve a higher CPU compute to GPU compute ratio like Ariel. AMD MI450 will be the most advanced GPU in the market in terms of silicon engineering: 2nm, hybrid bonding, 12 stacks of HBM, with the largest CoWoS reticle size on the market. This results in the highest compute and memory density per chip.
通过将计算和内存芯片砍半，其目标是像 Ariel 那样实现更高的 CPU 算力与 GPU 算力配比。就芯片工程而言，AMD MI450本应是市场上最先进的 GPU：采用2nm 制程、混合键合工艺、12层 HBM 堆叠，以及市场上最大的 CoWoS 光罩尺寸，从而实现每颗芯片最高的算力和内存密度。
