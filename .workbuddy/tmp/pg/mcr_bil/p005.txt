A funny example of the disfunction that is approachable to those not in the weeds, Meta doesn’t give most employees assigned desks; people who have been there over a year still scrounge daily for a workspace, where sometimes the desk has a monitor, sometimes the monitor has the wrong cable, and sometimes there is no monitor at all. Employees have formally applied for an assigned desk and been denied, even though desks sit empty everywhere.
一个即便是外行也能一眼看懂的荒诞例子是：Meta 并不为大多数员工分配固定工位，入职一年多的员工每天仍要抢工位——有时工位上有显示器，有时显示器接口不对，有时干脆没有显示器。有员工正式申请过固定工位，却遭到拒绝，尽管公司到处都有空置的工位。
Finally, the Rivos acquisition has created dissatisfaction among the preexisting Meta silicon team. Many Rivos engineers joined with higher compensation and titles without having the requisite responsibilities to justify such perks, and they were placed under existing Meta silicon employees. This resulted in decreased morale within the pre-existing team and also with incoming Rivos staff, who lack the power to make decisions. Since the beginning of this year, a number of Meta’s silicon engineers been leaving for startups or other well established companies like Arm and Nvidia.
最后，Rivos 收购还在 Meta 原有的芯片团队中引发了不满。许多 Rivos 工程师加入时拿到了更高的薪酬和职级，却并不具备与这些待遇相匹配的相应职责，而他们又被安排在 Meta 原有芯片员工之下。这使得原有团队的士气下滑，缺乏决策权的新加入 Rivos 员工士气也同样低落。自今年年初以来，已有不少 Meta 芯片工程师离职，前往初创公司或 Arm、Nvidia 这类成熟大厂。
As we first reported for clients, around 30% of the Rivos engineers who joined Meta as part of the acquisition were let go in recent layoffs. Rivos co-founder Mark Hayter has already left. A number of former Rivos people have since departed Meta to join Gerard Williams’s new chip startup, Nuvacore, after their first tranche of RSUs vested in May. We believe that Rivos CEO and co-founder, Puneet Kumar, is also eyeing up an exit in a year or two when his shares in Meta fully vest. There is speculation he could join Rosaic Labs, a new chip startup co-founded by Amarjit Gill, an investor in Rivos and longtime collaborator with Puneet Kumar at SiByte, P.A. Semi, Apple and Agnilux.
正如我们此前率先向客户报道的那样，随着这笔收购加入 Meta 的 Rivos 工程师中，约30%已在近期裁员中被辞退。Rivos 联合创始人 Mark Hayter 已经离职。5月首批限制性股票单位（RSU）归属后，多名前 Rivos 员工相继离开 Meta，加入了 Gerard Williams 新创立的芯片公司 Nuvacore。我们认为，Rivos 首席执行官兼联合创始人 Puneet Kumar 也在盘算一两年后、待其在 Meta 的股份全部归属后离职。据传他可能加盟 RosaicLabs——这是由 Amarjit Gill 联合创立的新芯片初创公司，Gill 本人是 Rivos 的投资人，也是 Puneet Kumar在 SiByte、P.A. Semi、Apple 和 Agnilux 时期的长期合作伙伴。
