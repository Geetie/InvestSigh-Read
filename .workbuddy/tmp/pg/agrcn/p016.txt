As we discuss below, there are implications regarding the phenomenon of agents helping users shop for insurance. But there is a new dimension to this as well - agents creating demand for embedded protection.
If you check out with an agent and something goes wrong, who’s on the hook? Well, it looks like - at least if you’re using Link on Muse - AIG.
Now, that could be a negative. We’ve seen before the disastrous impact of insurance acting in new segments like rideshare (look at James River for a great example). Still, we see this as one clear cut example where an insurer is benefitting from increased demand due to agents and moving fast enough to capture it. So, we consider AIG - right now - a beneficiary.
We’ll have to wait and see what the impact of the agents on the claims utilization is.
