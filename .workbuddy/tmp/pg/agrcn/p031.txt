That LTV estimate sets the ceiling on what a company can afford to spend on acquiring the customer. In industry terms: a customer acquisition cost, or CAC. The objective of consumer subscription businesses is to ensure that the LTV is an order of magnitude greater than the CAC.
The Agentic Reality asks the question: what happens when you remove friction from the subscription model? What if the lazy customer – through no effort of their own – suddenly became hypervigilant, and not only remembers to cancel their subscription, but is now looking to bargain for a better rate?
The use case for cutting subscriptions is salient enough to lure OpenAl back into the consumer market.
