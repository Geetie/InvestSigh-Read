Property Insurance: Revisiting Agent on Agent Violence
Property insurance falls in the same category. Customers still need insurance, and we’re all a little exhausted from having to shop around for the best rate. But, the annual renewal becomes a lot less arduous if the agent is automatically re-shopping the policy every year.
Yesterday l uploaded my auto insurance policy to Meta @Muse and asked for a better rate with identical coverage. In \~5 minutes, it found a policy saving me \$3,5oo /year, bought it, and canceled my old one.
I knew l was overpaying. I'd abandoned several attempts to switch because insurance websites and aggregator portals are so painful.
Why fill out forms, compare quotes,and field sales calls when an agent can finish the entire job in five minutes? Killer product.
This too, warrants a degree of nuance. Agencies operate on the asymmetry rent – while the direct carriers are likely to emerge from this structure with more control over the customer relationship and will maintain a greater share of the underwriting margin. By extension, the agencies see a shrinking degree of underwriting volume.
We’ll go long direct carriers – GEICO (owned by BRK/B US), Progressive (PGR US), and short a sleeve of companies like Allstate (ALL US), Travelers (TRV US), Hartford (HIG US), Hanover (THG US), and Selective (SIGI US).
