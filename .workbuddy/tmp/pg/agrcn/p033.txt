In our subscriber chat, we flagged The New York Times (NYT US) as a short candidate under this framework. Digital subscriptions account for a majority of NYT’s subscriber base, with promotional subscriptions driving a +3% boost to ARPU during their latest earnings release.
However, there is a plausible scenario in which subscriber numbers remain firm, but ARPU slips as agents negotiate retention discounts and trade down to standalone Games or Cooking subscriptions.
Competing publishers, however, may not have the quality nor durability necessary to protect their subscriber base.
People Incorporated (PPLI US), formerly Interactive Corp, is the US largest magazine publisher, owning the eponymous People Magazine, Travel+Leisure, as well as online publications like Investopedia.

The same applies to Future PLC (FUTR LN) which owns storied brands like Woman’ s Weekly and Practical Caravan. Ziff Davis (ZD US) faces a similar problem, owning a portfolio of Web 1.0 brands: CNET, IGN, and PCMag. USA Today (TDAY US) attributes roughly half of its revenue to digital. It’s a fine strategy for 2016, inflecting at exactly the wrong time.
