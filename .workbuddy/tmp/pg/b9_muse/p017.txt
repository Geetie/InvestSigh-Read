3. Driving GMV Growth Through Intent-Driven Ads
We conducted a particularly useful agency expert interview this week and strongly recommend that clients read the full transcript. We summarize the key points below.
Meta already understands shopping intent well. The harder problem is timing.
1 . People go to Google or Amazon because they want something now; they often open Facebook or Instagram because they are bored.
2 . Nearly all advertisers retarget using website or CRM data. If a user visits a Nike running-shoe page, Meta can infer that the user is shopping for running shoes and allow other brands to serve relevant ads.
3. Meta may identify the intent only seconds after Google, but its cost per thousand impressions (CPM) is lower because users did not come to the platform to shop.
4. Muse could materially expand that capability. Users may connect email, calendars, WhatsApp and other everyday apps to help manage their lives.
5. Meta could then identify events such as prenatal appointments, a move or half-marathon training and proactively help with nursery furnishings, a moving truck or running shoes.
6. Muse is designed to take the initiative rather than wait for someone to click an ad.
