Muse Use Cases by Category
The six largest primary categories are saving money, refunds and personal finance, with 128 cases (19.6%); work and small business, 73 (11.2%) ; administrative tasks, 69 (10.6%) ; shopping, 62 (9.5%) ; technology and agent setup, 54 (8.3%); and travel, 53 (8.1%). The remaining cases cover personal organization (34), health (29), food and dining (29) , family (28) , entertainment (22) , content creation (22) , research (15), social activities and home tasks (13 each), and other uses ( 10) .
