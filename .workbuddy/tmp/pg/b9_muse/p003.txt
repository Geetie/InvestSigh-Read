1. How People Are Using Muse
Twelve days after launch, the task users most want Muse to handle has not changed: arguing with companies on their behalf. Saving money, securing refunds and managing personal finances remain the largest category in our database, with 128 of 654 cases, or 19.6%. Phone calls appeared in just 26 cases in our first cut four days ago; that number has more than doubled to 58. The most-viewed post is now a phone-call story: a user had Muse negotiate with Comcast and streamline a mobile plan, saving more than \$800 a year. The post drew 230,000 views.
