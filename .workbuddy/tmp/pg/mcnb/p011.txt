Compute is the lifeblood of every AI research org. Anthropic would never sell compute to any of its competitors, and it’s therefore tempting to view Meta’s decision as admitting defeat for MSL.
算力是每一家 AI 研究机构的命脉。Anthropic 绝不会把算力卖给任何竞争对手，因此人们很容易把 Meta 的这个决定读成 MSL 认输。
However, we believe this framing is too simplistic. There is a world where you can temporarily sell compute to your fiercest competitors AND still become a true frontier lab. Like so much else we’ve covered in this article, Zuck really needs to thank Elon for showing him this playbook.
但我们认为这个框架过于简单。存在这样一种可能：你一边把算力临时卖给最凶狠的竞争对手，一边仍然长成一家真正的前沿实验室。就像本文中我们讲过的许多事情一样，扎克伯格真该好好谢谢马斯克，是他把这套打法演示了一遍。
We believe the following is the most likely explanation for the SpaceX/Cursor acquisition:
对于 SpaceX 收购 Cursor 一事，我们认为下面是最可能的解释：
Elon and Cursor are both still serious about building superintelligence. Cursor’s messaging over the past 6 months has changed from “we want to build the best AI coding experience” to “we want to build RSI”. This is the ethos of a frontier lab, not a wrapper startup.
SpaceX still has up to 900MW left after their eye-watering compute deals. This is sufficient for taking a few true shots on goal at reaching the frontier. Both GPT 5.6 and Fable 5 were likely trained using less compute.
The 90-day bilateral cancellation in all the SpaceX compute deals is crucial! Many were viewing this as a win for Anthropic/Google—because they can just cancel the deal if they find something cheaper—but it also means Elon can claw back all his compute if the Cursor team does well!
In other words, SpaceX/Cursor still meet the minimum requirements for training a frontier model. Over the next 6 months, we will see if they can deliver on that potential. If they do, they will claw back all their compute and continue scaling to push the frontier. If they fail, then they’ll give up for real and become a very large and profitable CSP instead. That’s certainly not a bad backup plan, and the only true cost will have been the unmonetized GPU hours spent chasing the dream of RSI.
马斯克和 Cursor 都仍然认真地想造出超级智能。过去 6 个月里，Cursor 的对外口径已经从“我们要做最好的 AI 编程体验”变成了“我们要做 RSI（递归自我改进）”。这是前沿实验室的信条，不是套壳创业公司的。
在那些令人咋舌的算力交易之后，SpaceX 手上仍留有多达 900MW。这足够它对着前沿真正射门几次了。GPT 5.6 和 Fable 5 用掉的算力很可能都比这更少。
SpaceX 所有算力交易里的 90 天双向取消条款至关重要！很多人把它看作 Anthropic/Google 的胜利—因为一旦找到更便宜的选项就可以直接毁约——但它同样意味着：只要 Cursor 团队做得好，马斯克可以随时把算力全部收回！
换句话说，SpaceX/Cursor 仍然满足训练一个前沿模型的最低门槛。未来 6 个月，我们就能看到它们能否兑现这份潜力。如果能，它们会把算力全部收回，继续扩展、冲击前沿。如果不能，那它们就是真的放弃了，转而成为一家规模巨大且盈利丰厚的云服务商（CSP）。这当然算不上一个糟糕的备胎方案，唯一的真实代价，不过是追逐 RSI 之梦时那些未被变现的 GPU 工时。
