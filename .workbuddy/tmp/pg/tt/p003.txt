2. TikTok is now offering premium inventory
a. If you have heard of TikTok Pulse, you can buy on TikTok in a non-auction format, similar to how you would pre-buy TV inventory.
b. “Hey TikTok, I sell cosmetics, I want to buy the top five TikTok channels related to people doing makeup, or some equivalent inventory.” They have all the permutations: TikTok Lineup, TikTok Pulse, available for advertisers to purchase upfront. As a result, they are pulling in significant brand budgets, whereas they were historically known for mid-funnel and lower-funnel spend (conversion, consideration, viral lift). Now they can capture sizable brand budgets through TikTok Pulse. This has been working well, and Pulse inventory has been selling out frequently. The fact that Pulse is sold out tells you there is real demand for this kind of inventory.

a. Yes, absolutely. As I said, TikTok will compete head-to-head with Meta. I think it competes more with Meta than with Google because of how the budgets are categorized.
b. Google is search, Google is AI, Google through YouTube is TV. These are completely different experiences. Google has a massive data advantage as a single ecosystem, and provides analytics tools and everything else to advertisers.
Fundamentally, Google is too powerful, which is why they have antitrust issues; they own the entire value chain.
c. Meta is executing well and has solid growth momentum this year, but TikTok is a real competitor because they are launching different products, and as I noted, we are starting to see TikTok poach sales talent from Meta to sell for them. So they are going after Meta’s budget. Yes, this is a threat to Meta.
