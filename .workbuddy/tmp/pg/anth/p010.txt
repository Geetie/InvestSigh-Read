Anthropic's revenue composes several product areas across both subscription and API usage-based pricing. For subscriptions,the company has several business lines: Consumer, Team,and Enterprise.
Anthropic 的收入由订阅和基于API使用量定价的多个产品领域组成。在订阅方面,该公司拥有多条业务线：个人版、团队版和企业版。
Consumer is split into Free, Pro, Max 5x,and Max 20x with pricing on the paid plans \$20, \$100,and \$200/month respectively when billed monthly. Claude Code, Cowork, and features like Claude for Microsoft 365 are included on all paid plans. Our analysis of card panel data suggests that the average paying US consumer is spending around \$45/month on Claude. Team subscriptions are for SMBs between 5-150 seats and are \$20 or \$100/month when billed annually.
消费端分为免费版、Pro版、Max 5x版和Max 20x版，付费套餐按月订阅的价格分别为每月20美元、100美元和200 美元。所有付费套餐均包含ClaudeCode、Cowork以及Claude forMicrosoft365 等功能。我们对银行卡面板数据的分析显示，美国付费用户平均每月在Claude上的支出约为45美元。团队订阅面向5至150个席位的中小企业，按年付费时价格为每月20美元或100美元。
Both consumer and team subscriptions have an opaque usage-based system for burning tokens. SemiAnalysis work on the equivalent API token spend showed that subscriptions provide much more usage than the cost for the equivalent task load on API, but Anthropic has the ultimate say on when and how much usage may be throttled.
消费端和团队订阅均采用不透明的基于使用量的令牌消耗机制。半分析团队对等效API令牌支出的研究显示，相比API上的同等任务量成本，订阅提供了更多使用额度，但Anthropic 对何时以及何种程度的使用可能被限速拥有最终决定权。
For enterprise subscriptions,however, no usage is included for the subscription price.After 150 seats on the Team plan, you are required to pay \$20/month plus the API ratesfor allusage.
