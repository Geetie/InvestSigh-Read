By focusing on API revenue, there is no ceiling on how much revenue can be generated from a single user because it's billed by token rather than by seat. As the same user/customer adopts more agentic workflows, they consume more tokens and generate more revenue without Anthropic needing to add another customer. The disclosures show this. Anthropic CFO Krishna Rao shared on the Invest Like the Best podcast in early May that Net Dollar Retention, the measure of how much spend has increased at customers who were with the company at least 1 year prior, was 500%. This means \$12B of Anthropic's \$30B of 1Q26 ARR was driven by the customers who were just \$2B of ARR in 1Q25. This means there is still \$18B of new customer ARR added over the last year who are stil ramping their spend and powering Anthropic's growth higher.
通过聚焦API收入，单个用户能带来的收入没有上限，因为计费方式是按 token 而非按席位。当同一用户/客户采用更多智能体工作流时，他们会消耗更多token并产生更多收入，而Anthropic 无需新增客户。披露信息证实了这一点。Anthropic 首席财务官克里希纳·拉奥在5月初的《InvestLike the Best》播客中透露，净美元留存率（衡量与公司合作至少一年的客户支出增长指标）达到 500%。这意味着 Anthropic 2026年第一季度 300亿美元ARR中，有120亿美元来自 2025年第一季度仅贡献 20亿美元ARR 的客户群体。而过去一年新增的180 亿美元ARR客户仍在持续增加支出，推动Anthropic 实现更高增长。
OpenAI is improving, however. The SemiAnalysis Tokenomics team believes that API has been responsible for driving the reacceleration in OpenAI ARR since the March release of 5.5 and Codex and B2B API are now the majority of monthly NNARR. This revenue is higher in margin and could help OpenAI reach profitability sooner than expected.
不过OpenAI也在进步。SemiAnalysis 代币经济学团队认为，自3月发布5.5和Codex 以来，API已成为OpenAIARR 重新加速的核心驱动力，B2BAPI目前占月度新增ARR的大部分。这类收入利润率更高，可能帮助OpenAI比预期更早实现盈利。

With higher token pricing on new models, how has blended pricing gone down? Cache rates, input/output ratios,and agentic vs chat workflow mix are all factors in determining blended token pricing.
