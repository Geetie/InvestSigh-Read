Our house view is that Fable from both a pricing mix per M Tokens and expanded use case perspective will help accelerate Anthropic monthly NNARR above its current \$10B/month pace in 2H6.
我们的内部观点是，无论是从每百万Token的定价组合角度，还是从用例扩展的角度来看，Fable 都将有助于推动Anthropic 在 2026 年下半年将月度NNARR 超越当前每月100亿美元的增长速度。
The number of customers spending over \$100k/year on Claude has grown 7x in the past year, and customers spending over \$1M /year up roughly 42x over the past two years. When we look at the SaaS and Cloud optimization phase in 2022 and 2023, we see the hit to multiples and growth rates that can occur. Many of these businesses like Snowflake, Datadog, and Cloudflare traded at ridiculous multiples (5Ox forward revenue or more!) and growth sharply decelerated. We don't see the major risks to that sort of optimization in the short term. The Global 2000/Fortune 500, who make up a large amount of total IT spend,are so nascent today in their usage of frontier AI products that the ramp period in terms of seat deployments and per seat usage are major factors in 2H26 and 2027 NNARR growth. In addition, new use cases are being unlocked every day, and we expect what Claude Code did for growth will be repeated at a higher and faster pace with Mythos and cybersecurity. Healthcare, finance, biotech, and more verticals will all have their moments of massive TAM workload expansion and aggressive growth rates up those s-curves.
过去一年，年消费超10万美元的Claude客户数量增长了7倍，年消费超100万美元的客户在过去两年间更是激增约42倍。回顾2022至 2023年的 SaaS与云优化阶段,我们看到了倍数与增长率可能遭受的冲击。当时的 Snowflake、Datadog、Cloudflare等企业，其交易倍数高得离谱（远期营收可达50倍以上！），而增长速度却急剧放缓。短期内我们并未看到类似优化对行业构成重大威胁。在全球IT支出占比极高的全球 2000 强/财富 500 强企业中，前沿AI产品的使用仍处于萌芽阶段，因此席位部署量与人均使用量将是2026下半年及2027年NNARR增长的关键驱动因素。此外，新应用场景正不断涌现—一我们预计ClaudeCode对增长的推动作用，将在Mythos 和网络安全领域以更高强度、更快速度重现。医疗、金融、生物科技等垂直行业，都将迎来其 TAM工作量的大规模扩张，并在增长曲线上呈现激进的上扬态势。
