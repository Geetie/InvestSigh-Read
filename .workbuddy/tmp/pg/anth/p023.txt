Power users at power customers drive lab revenue today. The Tokenomics team estimates that over 65% of Lab ARR today is from coding use cases and a host of other coding startups such as Cursor, Cognition, Loveable, Replit, and more compose a additional \$6B of ARR as of 2Q26.
