Turning to chip startups for help
As Maia vs OpenAI/Broadcom vs NVIDIA turns into an open question and Microsoft begins to diversify its chip vendors, we believe they will also turn to startups for help across the stack.
Specifically, MI2, Microsoft’s Venture fund, recently posted a photo promoting an event that featured Modular, Neurophos, and other startups. Modular is a software startup working on programming frameworks and inference for diverse accelerators beyond NVIDIA, while Neurophos is a chip startup working on an Optical Processing Unit (again, taking on NVIDIA). If successful, companies like this would represent some of the pieces needed to take on NVIDIA: Modular MAX replacing inference runtimes like vLLM and SGLang, while Neurophos takes on the chips themselves with their OPU attaching superlatives like “1000x more FLOPs per pJ”.
