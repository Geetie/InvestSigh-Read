External customers are ordering significant volumes of TPU next year. Anthropic is one of them having jointly announced with Google that they will order at least 1mn TPUs for next year. You can subscribe to the Accelerator Model to find out who are the other major customer external TPU customers and the volumes. Overall, Google continues to set the bar for hyperscaler AI chips. It is one thing to no longer need merchant GPUs for internal workloads, it is another to have multiple sophisticated customers also want to use this ASIC as well. Amazon is shipping millions of units of its Trainium line of accelerators, with Anthropic as the anchor customer for Trainium2, accounting for virtually the entire Trainium2 program. It is this ramp of Amazon’s Trainium2 clusters for Anthropic is what is driving AWS’s big revenue re-acceleration which we correctly called. Interestingly, this means that nearly all of Amazon’s Trainium demand is external. In fairness, a large part of this is that Amazon has the least interest amongst the hyperscalers in developing their own frontier models and is content with being a pure infrastructure provider. This is more damning on Microsoft’s efforts: Amazon has already graduated to being a merchant AI systems provider. They will make significant gross profits from renting out their own AI hardware and they will do this without having much in the way of internal workloads to dogfood the hardware and software stack on. This goes against Satya’s argument that “you better have your own model” to underpin the demand for an ASIC effort. Instead, it is Anthropic who brings the model and who played a big role in designing the overall system and is committed to getting it to work to lower TCO and provide more inference and post-training compute.
