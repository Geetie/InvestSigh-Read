The One Big Beautiful Bill (OBBB) Act (H.R.1, 119th Congress), signed into law by the Trump administration on July 4, 2025, introduces important tax reforms that are favorable to datacenter developers and hyperscale infrastructure investors such as Meta. The OBBB gives a boost to hyperscaler CapEx by restoring 100% bonus depreciation and full R&D expensing, cutting tax burdens on datacenter builds and GPU purchases. Expanded interest deductibility and enhanced Section 179 incentives further improve cash flow for large datacenter projects. While clean energy credits are phased out and compliance burdens rise, the bill clearly favors owning over renting compute, giving CapEx-heavy hyperscalers a long-term advantage.
《One Big Beautiful Bill》（OBBB）法案（第 119 届国会 H.R.1），由特朗普政府于 2025 年 7 月 4 日 签署成法，引入了对数据中心开发商及 Meta 这类超大规模基础设施投资者有利的重要税制改革。OBBB 通过恢复100% 加速折旧（bonus depreciation）和全额研发费用即时抵扣，减轻了数据中心建设与 GPU 采购的税负，从而提振了超大规模云厂商的资本开支。利息抵扣范围的扩大以及 Section 179 激励的增强，进一步改善了大型数据中心项目的现金流。尽管清洁能源抵免被逐步取消、合规负担上升，但该法案明显更偏向“自有算力”而非“租用算力”，从而给资本开支密集的超大规模云厂商带来长期优势。
Favorable tax incentives and cost recovery for datacenter buildout
有利于数据中心建设的税收激励与成本回收
100% bonus depreciation restored through 2030: Full first-year write-off for servers, electrical, and cooling gear. R&D expensing restored retroactively to 2022: Immediate deduction for AI model training and development. Interest deductibility expanded (back to EBITDA-based): Helps debt-financed datacenter projects. Section 179D (energy-efficient building deduction) remains through 2026, then sunsets. Impact: Significantly improves cash flow and ROI for new builds; shortens payback periods by 1–2 years vs. pre-OBBB.
100% 加速折旧恢复并延续至 2030 年：服务器、电气与制冷设备可在第一年全额抵扣。研发费用即时抵扣追溯恢复至 2022 年：AI 模型训练与开发支出可即时抵扣。利息抵扣范围扩大（重新以 EBITDA 为基准）：有助于以债务融资的数据中心项目。Section 179D（节能建筑抵扣）保留至 2026 年，之后到期终止。影响：显著改善新建项目的现金流与 ROI（投资回报率）；相比 OBBB 之前，回本周期缩短 1—2 年。
