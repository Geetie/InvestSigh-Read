By halving the compute and memory silicon, the goal is to achieve a higher CPU compute to GPU compute ratio like Ariel. AMD MI450 will be the most advanced GPU in the market in terms of silicon engineering: 2nm, hybrid bonding, 12 stacks of HBM, with the largest CoWoS reticle size on the market. This results in the highest compute and memory density per chip.
Meta choosing to cut this engineering marvel in half blunts the proposition completely. This chip configuration is intended for Recsys workloads and was something that Recsys infrastructure teams decided. However, the decision was made before TBD Lab was formed or could have its say. Given the significant compute and HBM deficiency for the scale up domain versus Rubin, TBD has no interest in this system, nor is it attractive for external customers.
