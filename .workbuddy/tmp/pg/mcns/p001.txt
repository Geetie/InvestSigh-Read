Meta Compute: Everyone Wants To Be A Neocloud Meta 的算力:人人都想做 Neocloud
SemiAnalysis · 2026-07-02
★ Meta turning Neocloud is not a retreat — it is what makes an accelerating buildout safe, because four high-margin ways to sell compute (frontier models, RecSys, a Bedrock-style Claude endpoint, and SpaceX-style premium on-demand deals) mean Meta can keep contracting aggressively whether or not MSL succeeds.
Meta 做 Neocloud 不是撤退，而是让激进扩产变得安全的前提:卖算力有四条高毛利路径（前沿模型、推荐系统、Bedrock 式的Claude 接口、SpaceX 式高溢价按需交易），无论 MSL（Meta Superintelligence Labs，Meta 超级智能实验室）成败，Meta都能继续激进锁定算力。
